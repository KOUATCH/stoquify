# AqStoqFlow / Stoquify blocker-gate module — value decision and next actions

Prepared: 2026-08-19  
Decision: **BUILD_G1_PILOT_ONLY**  
Implementation performed: no  
Production authorization: false

## Outcome

Build a narrow Phase 0–2 G1 pilot inside the existing Workflow Assurance module. Do not approve a platform-wide rollout, standalone microservice or complete internal legal e-signature platform yet.

This is the right next move because:

- G1 has a real, current workflow gap: 13/13 technical checks pass, but 0/11 decisions and 0/33 role obligations have authentic approval evidence.
- Stoquify already has most foundations needed for a safe pilot: tenant-aware protection, fresh authentication, assurance incidents/alerts/waivers, evidence services, business events/outbox and control-tower UI.
- The missing authority, approval, evidence-envelope and independent-verification capabilities are technically feasible and reusable.
- Platform-wide ROI is not proven because real workload, labor, audit, incident, sales and willingness-to-pay data is absent.
- A fixed pilot can solve the immediate workflow and measure the missing economic evidence while the current G1 script remains fail closed.

## Required authorization before coding

1. Name the owner of the authoritative governance-role roster.
2. Approve stable G1 authority codes, scopes and delegation rules.
3. Approve same-person and segregation-of-duties rules.
4. Define accepted qualification evidence for Cameroon and accounting reviewers.
5. Approve evidence retention, redaction and export classes.
6. Decide whether internal authenticated attestation is sufficient or any G1 item requires an external e-signature provider.
7. Approve fresh-authentication assurance and sensitive permissions.
8. Independently review the additive schema and rollback design.

Do not derive these values from display names, ordinary application roles or the existing handwritten images.

## Authorized pilot scope after prerequisites

- Register and hash the exact frozen G1 artifact.
- Import D-01 through D-11 and assert exactly 33 role obligations.
- Implement authority/delegation, authenticated approval, attestation and evidence-envelope records.
- Implement independent verification and drift invalidation.
- Generate a verified G1 manifest in observe mode for comparison with the existing gate.
- Add only the minimal G1 authority, inbox, verification and evidence UI.
- Test tenant isolation, replay, stale auth, wrong hashes/options, SoD, self-verification, accessibility and recovery.

## Keep out of scope

- No automatic or agent approval.
- No handwritten-image approval credit.
- No production secrets in evidence.
- No destructive migration or historical rewrite.
- No platform-wide domain migration.
- No legal or statutory signature claim.
- No production release or G1-pass claim during the pilot build.

## Investment checkpoint

The illustrative base model estimates:

- three-year pilot effort: 22.75 role-months / 3,640 hours;
- three-year direct labor savings: about 1,419 hours;
- direct labor recovery: about 39%, before infrastructure, provider and qualified-review costs.

These are assumptions, not a budget. The uncovered value must be proven through actual release-delay, incident, audit/procurement, customer or higher-volume workflow evidence. Full expansion is not authorized until measured risk-adjusted value covers measured TCO or an executive sponsor explicitly accepts the strategic gap.

## Pilot measurements

- build and review hours by work package;
- manual preparation/coordination baseline and reduction;
- median and p95 obligation age;
- correct first-time routing to confirmed authority;
- rework caused by invalid evidence;
- verification and invalidation latency;
- support, training, storage and operating cost;
- security/privacy defects and false blockers;
- second-domain adapter effort.

## Stop or defer when

- authority ownership is unresolved;
- any high-severity tenant or evidence-integrity defect remains open;
- the budget tolerance is breached;
- stabilized preparation/coordination improves by less than 20%;
- the authority model cannot represent real appointments without frequent manual repair;
- a second domain needs a parallel model; or
- external/customer evidence shows no material value beyond one internal gate.
