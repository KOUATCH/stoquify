## Minimum independent actors

| Workflow | Minimum distinct actors | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock adjustment/manual journal | 2 | Preparer and poster differ for each record. |
| Supplier payment | 3 | Requester, approver and execution recorder differ. |
| Payroll prepare/review/approve/post | 3 | Three actors through approval. Reviewer can technically post if separately permitted; not an appointment recommendation. |
| Payroll payment | 3 | Requester, approver and releaser differ. |
| Branch close | 2 | Review starter and signer differ; both need actual scope. |
| Certified close | 2 | Runner and certifier differ; evidence/review requirements can require additional people. |
| AI approvals | 2 | Product/security approvers differ; owner/backup coverage is an additional requirement. |

Three distinct operational people are the technical lower bound for each supplier-payment and payroll approval/release chain. That is not a claim that three people suffice for the entire enterprise. Specialists, POS decisions, branch coverage, backups and qualifications add responsibilities. The same properly authorized person may work across unrelated modules; nobody may approve their own transaction where the contract requires independence. Separate login IDs alone do not prove separate people. Missing optional creator IDs prevent proof of independence.
