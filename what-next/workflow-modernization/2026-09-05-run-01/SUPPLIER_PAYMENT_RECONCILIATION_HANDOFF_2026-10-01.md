## Operating the cash evidence handoff

1. Open the handoff from the recorded supplier payment. The payment must already have its real request, approval, execution evidence and posted journal. The server obtains its amount, currency, supplier and original exception; operators do not re-enter these values.
2. Obtain a separate recipient acknowledgment for that payment. Enter its voucher/reference number, recipient name, acknowledgment time with timezone, purpose and reviewer-accessible evidence URI. These come from the retained acknowledgment, not the original payment request or execution receipt. Select the final file to compute SHA-256 locally, or supply its actual hash. The file is not uploaded or remotely verified by this form; retain it in the organization's controlled evidence location.
3. Review the preview and verify your password when requested, then explicitly submit the proposal. A different authorized reconciliation reviewer checks the acknowledgment against the recorded payment and confirms the decision. The requester, payment approver, execution recorder and evidence proposer cannot approve this review.
4. Inspect the retained decision and refresh Close assessment before relying on a new Close export. “Cash receipt reviewed” means an authenticated internal review; it does not authenticate a payee signature or establish bank settlement.
5. For withdrawn or invalid evidence, use **Reopen**, supply the actual reason and confirm. Existing financial entries remain retained; the original reconciliation obligation reopens. Submit corrected evidence and obtain a new independent decision. If a response is lost, retry the retained request; do not create a replacement payment.
