## Operating inputs and ownership

Open the Close center for the required period. A person with evidence-request permission selects the actual historical missing finding, eligible response owner, due date in their local timezone and a clear explanation of the source needed. Obtain the finding from retained Close records and the owner from the colleagues responsible for that source. The assigned colleague supplies a response explaining the correction and source location, then a preparer runs a fresh assessment. A different authorized reviewer examines the response and replacement collection, writes a conclusion and confirms acceptance with fresh identity verification. Acceptance records the review; it does not itself certify the period or authenticate a third-party signature.
