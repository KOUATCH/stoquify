# Receiving accounting evidence

FA-CASH-COMMAND remains In progress. This is a correction at its purchasing/accounting handoff, not closure of receiving, AP or accounting setup. The preceding goal turn made verified progress on inventory direction and retry; this turn reproduced and corrected another financial-status defect.

## Evidence and correction

A real receipt created through preview/confirmation correctly recorded stock and retained a failed accounting batch. In a new isolated negative fixture, changing only that batch's status to POSTED made the receiving read model claim `posted` although no journal or accounting source link existed. The before receipt is `lanes/environment/receipt-posting-truth-before-8797687d-1948-4150-8101-1eb070dc5d59.json`. This was a false success claim, not an approval or an actual posting.

The read model now requires corroborating stock-event payload/hash evidence, one matching posted batch, one posted unreversed journal, a matching source link and source hash, consistent tenant/source/period/date/currency, balanced nonnegative lines and an inventory debit equal to the received stock value. Contradictory or incomplete evidence is `unverified`; ordinary failed batches retain `review_required`. Queries remain tenant-scoped and failures propagate. Reads do not repair or generate accounting records. Historical decisions retain their recorded outcome, while the workbench's current status reflects current evidence.

The new pure evidence predicate is used by the existing service; no UI, approval, permission or posting authority changed. Receipt lines may produce separate stock events, so verification covers the complete set of stock signatures and accepts the linked hash from that verified set. Accounting entry/source-link dates bind to the receipt date; stock-event recording time can differ by milliseconds and is not substituted for the business source date.

## Why the remaining setup gates were not papered over

The supplier-invoice default currently debits INVENTORY for the invoice net amount (`services/accounting/default-posting-rules.ts`, AP-SUPPLIER-INVOICE). Adding a receipt default that also debits INVENTORY would risk duplicate recognition when the invoice is posted. The current goods-receipt stock wrapper explicitly creates GOODS_RECEIPT_LEDGER_REVIEW. Its batch status is not proof of a completed receipt accounting producer.

The next implementation must define and enforce the receipt-to-invoice recognition contract: reconcile accepted receipt quantities, stock values and source evidence; distinguish legacy receipts already recognized by invoices; apply receipt accrual and invoice clearing exactly once; handle partial invoices, value differences, cancellation/reversal and blocked-posting recovery; and verify the full connected effects. Existing configured rules cannot silently be replaced. This is an engineering dependency, not a request for another signature or a missing commercial subscription.

CASH_DRAWER_CLOSE still records POS closing evidence without a generic close-rule consumer in the inspected path. EXPENSE_APPROVAL has a schema purpose but no approval-posting consumer found in the targeted production service/action search. Their applicability and supported lifecycle remain explicit engineering work. All three existing setup requirements remain effective. No gate was cleared with a fabricated template or synthetic approval.

## Operator guidance and rollout

Open the relevant order at `/en/dashboard/purchase-orders/<order-id>` or its `/fr/` equivalent, then the receiving-history tab. A stock-recorded label confirms a different outcome from accounting-posted. `Unverified` means the accounting evidence needs investigation; a POSTED batch flag alone is insufficient. The buyer/receiver supplies the receipt number and inspection record; the accountant obtains the linked batch, journal and source link from retained accounting records. If evidence is missing, preserve the receipt and stock history. Do not receive the goods again or change flags to force success.

No migration, deployment, live activation, external message or real payment occurred. Rollout can change unsupported green statuses to unverified. Review affected receipts with their accounting owner and retain the before/after evidence. Reverting the display check does not repair missing ledger effects; use targeted source recovery only, preserving other working-tree changes and finalized records.
