## User and operator guidance

Open `/en/dashboard/daily-digest` or `/fr/dashboard/daily-digest`. Select Manager/Responsable, inspect the signal, then use Command/Pilotage to open purchase orders. A pending order is only classified overdue when its recorded expected delivery timestamp has passed. Purchasing staff obtain the expected date from the supplier's agreed delivery confirmation and maintain it through the authorized purchase-order workflow. A corrected future date removes the observation after refresh; missing dates do not establish either lateness or timely delivery.

Read source limitations even when no observations appear. Administrators supply current source permissions through existing role settings; the digest cannot grant access. Failed data reads must be retried after source recovery, never replaced with invented zero balances. No new signature, provider credential or product approval is required for these read-model corrections.
