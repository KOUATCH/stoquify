Delivery implements DRAFT -> CONFIRMED/reserved -> DELIVERED/stock-and-cost posting -> COMPLETED/billing-and-receivable. Payment remains PENDING after billing. Commands bind payload and expected version; writes and existing domain owners share a transaction. Lot/serial/batch/expiry items, partial delivery and shipping billing are explicitly unsupported. Cancellation/release/return/reversal paths were not found in this owner and must be resolved before presenting the lifecycle as complete. The optional fiscal document deliberately uses SANDBOX and does not constitute statutory approval.

The service's no-report fallback invents openingBalance=200 and session=active, and does not derive cash-in/out evidence. These are source-confirmed defects, not runtime demonstrations.

The broader financial analytics service was read only at the shared sales loader and daily report owner; payroll/finance sections remain with their reviewing lane. Existing read-model snapshots are not assumed to certify revenue recognition, cash correctness or country applicability. Required corrections and verification continue under the full assignment.
