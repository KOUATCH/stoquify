## Finding and correction

Previously `approveSupplierInvoice` trusted a saved MATCHED/EXCEPTION label. The isolated before-run reproduced acceptance of six inconsistent cases: changed invoice total, changed line tax, missing received-goods state, a cross-organization invoice line, altered match variance and duplicate match records. These could produce incorrect liabilities or use ambiguous evidence. Graph node `purchasing_apcontrolworkbench_tsx`, community 25, was a historical navigation hint; current action and service source established the actual boundary.

Approval now retains the supplier-first/invoice lock order and locks the invoice lines, matches and related receipt/order records before reading them. It requires one match in the invoice's organization, consistent invoice/supplier/line relationships, no prior approval/posting pointers, no paid amount and no unsupported discount. Existing supplier-ledger or posting-batch evidence on an unapproved invoice requires reconciliation instead of a second liability.

The existing `buildInvoiceLinePlans` owner recalculates current supplier/receipt/order/item relationships, received quantity, unit costs, per-line tax and totals, signed price variance and absolute variance. During approval its new internal exclusion parameter omits only this invoice from prior reservations and then counts its lines once. Normal preparation still counts all existing reservations. Header totals, line tax/totals/status, match amounts, zero quantity variance and the exact-match policy must agree. A matched label cannot override contradictory arithmetic or source evidence.

An active exception must cover the recomputed variance and current policy, belong to the original maker, have an independent approver and have a non-future decision time. Existing expiration/resolution controls remain effective. Cancellation of an order's remaining work does not eliminate obligations for goods already received. A replay of an already approved invoice follows the existing one-liability path; blocked posting still requires the separate recovery workflow.

The source checks run before the approval claim, supplier balance update, liability, journal or approval event. They return actionable business errors without changing those records on rejection. No schema or migration is needed.
