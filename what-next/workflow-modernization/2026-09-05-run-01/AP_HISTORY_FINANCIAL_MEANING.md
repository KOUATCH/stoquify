# AP history financial meaning and recovery — v75

## What changed

The v74 counterexample showed a MATCHED document of 2,500 XAF as an open debt and positive payable movement, although its tenant had no supplier ledger rows. The history service now separates document value from recognized supplier-account movement. A clean unposted invoice remains visible with its original amount and explicit not-recognized text, while its recognized remainder is zero. Recorded movements require a matching, unique, same-organization/supplier ledger entry with the expected source, type, date, amount and direction. Missing or contradictory evidence is unavailable, never silently zero.

Current remainders of filtered recognized invoices subtract verified recorded payment allocations and supplier credit notes, including linked reversals. Stored paid amounts must reconcile with the recognized allocations. Archived sources remain visible with an archived/retained label and keep their obligations. Mixed currencies have separate totals; legacy scalar totals are null for mixed currency. Decimal calculations and presentation preserve cents. Old response shapes without the new financial basis are unavailable in the updated history and supplier dashboard.

The default read uses a RepeatableRead transaction. Organization-local calendar filters use the existing strict daily-period helper. These filters select documents; their current settlements can occur after the selected document-date interval. Changed records reject an older read boundary and require refresh. The cursor adapter is supplier-ap-history.v2; older cursors cannot be reused as new evidence.

## How to use it

Open /en/dashboard/purchases/payables/history or /fr/dashboard/purchases/payables/history through your existing authorized Purchases navigation. Select the document lane and date interval, then inspect the currency, document amount, recognized movement and retained marker. The date interval follows the displayed organization timezone. It is not a past-balance reconstruction.

Open Details for a record to inspect its operational references. Not recognized means the document has not established the verified supplier-account movement required by this view. Movement unverified or an unavailable total means evidence is missing or inconsistent; do not treat that amount as zero. Ask the AP owner to trace the source invoice/payment, its allocation or credit note, and the supplier ledger reference. Obtain these identifiers from the record detail and the underlying authorized invoice/payment workflow. Do not fabricate ledger rows to clear the display.

After a recoverable query error, choose Retry. Financial tiles are hidden during the failed result rather than presenting zeros. If a preview/cursor is stale, refresh the current history and reapply the intended filters. If permission is denied, an organization administrator must review the actual role grants; the interface cannot grant access. Existing aliases may convey AP viewing access, so removal of one alias alone is not full revocation.

The current remainder belongs only to filtered recognized invoices. It is not the complete supplier balance, an FX-converted organization total, a historical trial balance, a general-ledger posting certificate, or a statutory approval. Supplier liability and general-ledger posting remain distinct. A supplier liability can be recognized while downstream accounting setup remains blocked. Prepare export still has no completed artifact producer; this batch does not claim a downloadable or certified export.
