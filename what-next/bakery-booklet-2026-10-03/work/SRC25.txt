2. MASTER FROZEN & DAIRY MANUAL (CONDENSED MILK CORE)
Recipe #1: Commercial Soft-Serve Ice Cream Base
Ingredients: Sweetened Condensed Milk: 4.5 kg | Full-Cream Fresh/UHT Milk: 6 Liters | Heavy Cream (35% Fat): 3.5 Liters
| Clean Filtered Water: 6 Liters | Granulated Sugar: 1.0 kg | Commercial Ice Cream Stabilizer (Guar/Xanthan based): 80g |
Pure Vanilla Extract: 60ml.
Procedure (30L Jacketed Tank): Mix ingredients at Medium Speed. Heat to 85°C to pasteurize. Add sugar/stabilizer, mix
for 5 mins. Chill via cold water jacket circulation to 4°C. Age for 4 hours.
Recipe #2: High-Traffic 'Yaourt Caillé' & Frozen Yogurt Soft-Serve
Ingredients: Full-Fat Liquid Milk: 12 Liters | Sweetened Condensed Milk: 5.0 kg | Pure Water: 4 Liters | Live Active Yogurt
Starter Culture: 200g | Vanilla Blend: 40ml.


Procedure: Heat mix to 85°C for 15 mins. Cool via jacket down to 43°C. Pitch active starter culture. Let sit for 7-8 hours
until thick curd forms. For Frozen Yogurt, chill to 4°C and transfer directly to Soft-Serve Machine #2.
Recipe #3: Creamy Milky Popsicles (Esquimaux de Luxe)
Ingredients: Sweetened Condensed Milk: 6.0 kg | Water: 12 Liters | Coconut Milk or Mango Puree: 3 Liters | Cornstarch:
350g | Sea Salt: 15g.
Procedure: Cook water, condensed milk, and starch in tank at 75°C until thickened. Cool down. Pour into molds, submerge
into the popsicle brine machine at -22°C until solid.
3. THE 360° ULTIMATE COMMERCIAL SHAWARMA SYSTEM
Component A: Hollow Lebanese Flatbread (Khubz)
Ingredients: High-Gluten Baker's Flour: 5 kg | Water: 2.8 Liters | Sugar: 120g | Fine Salt: 80g | Vegetable Oil: 150ml | Active
Dry Yeast: 60g.
Procedure: Mix in Spiral Mixer for 9 mins. Divide into 100g balls. Run through Molder to flat 2mm discs. Proof for 20 mins.
Bake in Rotary Oven at 240°C for 90 seconds until hollow balloon forms.
Component B: High-Yield Massaged Shawarma Meat Marinade
Ingredients (per 10 kg sliced beef or chicken): Thick Strained Yogurt: 1.5 kg | Garlic Paste: 250g | White Vinegar: 200ml |
Vegetable Oil: 150ml | Sea Salt: 90g | Shawarma Spices: 150g.
Procedure (30L Mixing Tank): Place meat and ingredients into tank. Run scraper on Low Speed for 12 mins. The
massaging action breaks fibers and draws seasoning instantly.
Component C: Fast-Food Yogurt-Mayo Garlic Sauce (Toum-Express)
Ingredients: Premium Commercial Mayonnaise: 5.5 Liters | House yogurt: 3.5 Liters | Fresh Garlic: 500g | Lemon Juice:
250ml | White Vinegar: 100ml | Fine Salt: 40g | White Pepper: 15g.
Procedure: Mix garlic, juice, vinegar on High Speed. Drop to Medium Speed, add yogurt and spices. Finish on Low Speed
streaming in mayonnaise until thick.
Component D: Crunch-Preserved Pickled Cucumbers
Ingredients: Crisp Cucumbers: 5 kg | Boiling Water: 3 Liters | White Vinegar: 2 Liters | Sugar: 250g | Coarse Salt: 200g |
Garlic: 10 cloves.
Procedure: Bring water, vinegar, sugar, and salt to boil on Induction Cooker. Pour boiling brine over packed cucumbers in
buckets. Seal immediately.
Component E: Gourmet Ndjansang-Infused Spicy Chilli Sauce
Ingredients: Red Habanero Peppers: 2 kg | Ground Ndjansang Seeds: 150g | Garlic: 100g | Ginger: 100g | Vegetable Oil: 1
Liter | Salt: 40g.
Procedure: Simmer ingredients in Jacketed Tank at 100°C for 25 minutes while mixer spins slowly. Ndjansang thickens it
naturally with a nutty depth.

4. THE FAST-FOOD REVOLUTION: HAMBURGERS, PAIN CHARGÉ &
CHIPS
Recipe #9: Premium Sweetened Condensed Milk Hamburger Buns
Ingredients: Bread Flour: 6 kg | Sweetened Condensed Milk: 800g | Water: 2.8 Liters | Melted Butter: 500g | Whole Eggs: 6
| Salt: 90g | Instant Yeast: 80g | Sesame Seeds.
Procedure: Mix in Spiral Mixer for 9 mins. Divide into 90g portions. Round and place on trays. Proof for 50 mins at 38°C.
Egg wash, add sesame, bake in Rotary Oven at 190°C for 12 mins.
Recipe #10: The High-Traffic Nkambe Smash Burger & Assembly
Execution: Toast sliced bun. Ball 120g minced beef, smash completely flat on a hot induction griddle plate. Cook 90
seconds, flip, apply cheddar cheese. Assemble with a heavy layer of Yogurt-Mayo sauce, crunchy pickles, and onions.
Recipe #11: Pain Chargé Spiced Beef Filling (Bulk Tank Method)
Ingredients: Minced Beef: 4 kg | Diced Onions: 2 kg | Garlic & Ginger: 150g | White Pepper & Seasoning | Vegetable Oil:
300ml | Shredded Green Cabbage: 3 kg.
Procedure (30L Jacketed Tank): Heat jacket to high. Add oil, meat, onions, spices. Set mixer to Medium Speed. Cook until
browned. Reduce heat, drop in shredded cabbage, let scraper paddle integrate it until wilted.
Recipe #12: Industrial Double-Fried Crispy Potato & Plantain Chips
Procedure: Slice potatoes or plantains evenly. Rinse potato chips to strip starch. Blanch in Induction Fryer at 140°C for 5
minutes. Remove and drain. Flash fry a second time at 180°C for 2-3 minutes until golden crisp.
Recipe #13: Complete Pain Chargé Assembly with Meat, Chips & Sauce
The Assembly Architecture: Slice open a hot 30cm Pain Chargé Baguette. Slather generously with Yogurt-Mayo Garlic
Sauce. Pack with 150g of hot Spiced Beef Filling from the mixing tank. Stuff a handful of double-fried Crispy Chips over the
meat. Top with a drizzle of Ndjansang Chilli Sauce.
5. MASS-VOLUME PREMIUM BAKERY PRODUCTS
Recipe #14: High-Yield Milk Sandwich Bread (Pain de Mie Royal)
Ingredients: Hard Wheat Flour: 10 kg | Sweetened Condensed Milk: 1.2 kg | Water: 5.4 Liters | Shortening: 400g | Sea Salt:
150g | Yeast: 100g.
Procedure: Mix in Spiral Mixer for 10 mins. Form cylinders via Molder. Place in pullman pans. Proof for 60 mins at 38°C.
Bake in Rotary Oven at 200°C for 28 minutes.
Recipe #15: Hard-Crust Route Baguette (Pain Chargé Base)
Ingredients: Baker's Flour: 10 kg | Sweetened Condensed Milk: 300g | Water: 5.8 Liters | Fine Salt: 180g | Yeast: 90g.
Procedure: Mix until elastic. Shape via mechanical molder. Score 4 deep cuts across the top. Proof for 45 mins. Bake in
Rotary Oven at 220°C with full Steam Injection for 22 minutes.

Recipe #17: Golden Sweet Doughnuts (Beignets Soufflés Premium)

Recipe #18: Air-Whipped Light Industrial Koki Corn Cakes
Ingredients: Fresh Corn Mash: 10 kg | Palm Oil: 1.2 Liters | Habanero: 50g | Water: 1.5 Liters | Salt: 80g.
Procedure: Load corn mash and water into tank (No heat). Run scraper on Max Speed for 12 minutes to fold air. Stream in
palm oil and salt. Wrap and steam using Induction Cookers.
Recipe #19: Rich Caramel Topping Syrup
Ingredients: White Sugar: 4 kg | Sweetened Condensed Milk: 2.0 kg | Liquid Glucose: 1.5 kg | Water: 1 Liter | Butter: 250g.
Procedure: Cook water, sugar, glucose to amber stage (160°C). Lower heat, mix on Medium Speed while slowly adding
condensed milk and butter. Yields smooth drizzle.
Recipe #20: Residual Heat Sweet Coconut Pastry Slices
Procedure: Mix 2kg grated coconut and 1.5kg condensed milk. Spread onto pastry sheets. Load into Rotary Oven right
after turning off morning bread shifts. Residual heat bakes it to perfection with zero fuel cost.
