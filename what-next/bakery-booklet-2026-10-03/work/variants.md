These development records preserve distinct products and important formula differences from earlier drafts. They are not production instructions. Choose a complete primary card where available; otherwise resolve missing specifications, make a small controlled trial, measure yield and issue a full approved card. Original units and ranges remain visible so uncertainty is not hidden by invented conversions.

Older versions of the same product have been consolidated by family. Exact duplicates and superseded generic guidance are accounted for in the editorial record. Marketing claims about months of life, guaranteed profit, zero fuel cost or machinery performance have not been carried into working instructions.

Shokupan original: tangzhong flour 7% plus liquid 35%; main dough flour 93%, milk 50%, sugar 10%, butter 10%, egg 10%, instant yeast 1.5%, salt 1.2%. ## V08 — Brioche format options retained

SRC05’s extended brioche uses the same 50%-butter base concept as S-B06 and adds useful alternative shapes. A 5 kg flour batch has 11,450 g raw dough; at an assumed 2% handling loss, 11,221 g remains. Alternative allocations are 187 × 60 g with 1 g remainder, 140 × 80 g with 21 g remainder, 37 × 300 g with 121 g remainder, or 22 × 500 g with 221 g remainder. These are alternatives, not simultaneous outputs.

For a 500 g loaf, arrange five 100 g balls; for a three-strand braid, use 167 + 167 + 166 g. A brioche à tête may reserve about one fifth for its head. Trial small pieces at 170–180°C for 14–20 minutes, 300 g loaves at 165–175°C for 22–30 minutes, and 500 g loaves at 165–175°C for 30–40 minutes. Confirm crumb set and measured bake loss; the 90–94°C crumb target is a quality check, not a substitute for a validated food-safety process. Raw egg wash is applied only before baking.

Cool plain brioche completely before bagging. A frozen-quality trial at −18°C may compare two- and four-week samples; this does not establish a selling period or validate freezing raw dough. Keep butter, egg wash, refrigeration, rejects and unsold portions in the cost model.
