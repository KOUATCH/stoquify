Commercial Bakery Production Blueprint &
Recipe Manual
Location Focus: Nkambe, Donga-Mantung Division, North West Region, Cameroon
Strategic Advantage: Cold climate adaptation, energy load balancing, and high-margin product optimization.
1. Electrical Load & Infrastructure Management
Total connected load is 30.0 KW. Operating all equipment simultaneously will blow standard lines and incur massive demand
charges. Implement a strict staggered production schedule:
 Shift 1 (02:00 - 07:00): Spiral Mixer (6.5KW) + Molder (1.0KW) + Proofer (1.0KW) + Rotary Oven (3.0KW) = 11.5 KW max load.
 Focus: Breads.
 Shift 2 (08:00 - 13:00): Jacketed Tank (2.0KW) + Induction Fryers (5.0KW) + Induction Cookers (5.0KW) = 12.0 KW max load.
 Focus: Fryers and Koki.
 Shift 3 (16:00 - 21:00): Shawarma Machine (3.5KW) + Rotary Oven (3.0KW) + Induction Fryers (5.0KW) = 11.5 KW max load.
 Focus: Fast Food.
2. Twenty (20) High-Value Commercial Formulations
1. High-Yield Milk Sandwich Bread (Pain de Mie)
Equipment Workflow: Spiral Mixer -> Molder -> Proofer -> Rotary Oven
Ingredient
Commercial Batch Qty
Flour (T55)
50 kg
Water
28 L
Milk Powder
2.5 kg
Margarine
2 kg
Sugar
3 kg
Salt
900 g
Yeast
600 g
Commercial Method: Mix all solids in spiral mixer for 2 mins. Add water gradually. Mix on speed 2 for 8 mins to maximum gluten
development. Pass dough through the molder, cut into 500g pieces, place in oiled tins, proof at 38 C for 90 mins. Bake in Rotary
Oven at 210 C with steam injection for 26 mins.
Nkambe Market Edge: Milk solids hold moisture perfectly in Nkambe's cold mountain air, preventing staling and extending shelf life
to 4 full days.

2. Hard-Crust Baguette (Pain Chargé)
Equipment Workflow: Spiral Mixer -> Molder -> Proofer -> Rotary Oven
Ingredient
Commercial Batch Qty
Flour (High Gluten)
50 kg
Water
31 L
Salt
1 kg
Yeast
500 g
Ascorbic Acid
10 g
Commercial Method: Mix ingredients in spiral mixer on low for 4 mins, then high for 5 mins. Rest dough for 20 mins. Shape into tight
baguettes via molder. Proof on linen couches for 75 mins at ambient temperature. Score deeply. Bake in Rotary Oven at 230 C with
heavy initial steam for 22 mins.
Nkambe Market Edge: High hydration and dense structure give laborers and long-distance drivers the heavy, filling bread they
demand.

Commercial Method: Develop a high-hydration, sticky batter in the spiral mixer. Allow to ferment in a warm room for 60 mins until

5. Thick 'Yaourt Caillé' (Drinking Yogurt)
Equipment Workflow: 30L Jacketed Mixing Tank
Ingredient
Commercial Batch Qty
Full Cream Milk Powder
5 kg
Water
22 L
Sugar
2 kg
Yogurt Culture (YF-L811)
5 g
Flavoring Essence
50 ml
Commercial Method: Combine milk powder, sugar, and water in the tank. Heat via electric jacket to 85 C and hold for 15 mins to
pasteurize. Circulate cool water to drop temperature to 43 C. Pitch culture. Stir for 1 min, turn off mixer, cover and let incubate for 7
hours. Agitate slowly on low speed to smooth out.
Nkambe Market Edge: Using a closed sterile tank ensures absolute batch consistency and eliminates graininess, drawing
high-margin family packs.

Commercial Method: Load spices, oil, yogurt, and garlic paste into the mixing tank. Blend at medium speed for 3 mins until perfectly

8. High-Yield Commercial Meat Pie Filling
Equipment Workflow: 30L Jacketed Mixing Tank
Ingredient
Commercial Batch Qty
Minced Beef
4 kg
Fine Diced Potatoes
5 kg
Fine Diced Carrots
3 kg
Onions
2 kg
Thickening Starch
500 g
Seasoning / Water
6 L
Commercial Method: Turn on jacket heating. Sauté onions and minced beef using the scraper paddle. Add carrots, potatoes, water,
and seasoning. Simmer at 95 C with continuous scraping until veggies are tender. Add starch slurry to bind into a thick, glossy gravy.
Cool completely before stuffing pastry.
Nkambe Market Edge: Extending the meat base with uniform root vegetables cuts protein costs by 40% while preserving a rich,
moist mouthfeel.
9. Flaky Pastels / Meat Pie Crust
Equipment Workflow: Spiral Mixer -> Rotary Oven
Ingredient
Commercial Batch Qty
Flour
15 kg
Cold Margarine
7.5 kg
Salt
200 g
Ice Cold Water
4.5 L
Commercial Method: Add flour, salt, and cold margarine chunks to the spiral mixer. Run on low speed for 3 mins until a sandy
texture forms. Pour in ice-cold water all at once, mixing for just 90 seconds until a rough dough forms (do not overdevelop). Chill, roll,
cut, stuff, and bake at 200 C for 22 mins.
Nkambe Market Edge: Short mixing times preserve tiny fat pockets, yielding a premium shatteringly flaky crust that commands a
luxury price.

14. Rich Caramel Topping Syrup

Ingredient
Commercial Batch Qty

10 kg
Liquid Glucose
3 kg
Water
4 L
Heavy Cream
2 L

30 g
Commercial Method: Combine sugar, glucose, and water in the jacketed tank. Turn heat to 120 C. Allow to melt and caramelize into
