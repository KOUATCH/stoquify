Deep, amber-colored glossy caramel sauce to drizzle over your yogurt or fill specialty bakery pastries.

Tank Operational Method: Load sugar, water, and glucose into the jacketed tank. Turn heat on high. Do not engage
mixer until sugar liquifies. Once it hits a rich amber color, turn mixer on Low and carefully stream in cream and salt. Mix

■ Value Tip & Trick: Liquid glucose is vital. It acts as an anti-crystallization agent, ensuring your caramel stays
completely fluid, smooth, and glossy for weeks without turning gritty.
