SECTION 2: THE 20 POPULAR PRODUCT
HIGH-PROFITABILITY PORTFOLIO
To establish total market dominance across Douala and Yaoundé, you must offer the key high-margin sweet,
savory, and local staple lines that drive massive daily cash flow. Below are the precise technical details,
commercial recipes, and tricks for 20 essential products.
Product 1: Premium Kumba Bread (High Quality Local Loaf)
Market Strategy & Niche: The most legendary heavy, dense, sweet loaf across Littoral and Centre provinces.
Local bakeries fail consistency; your industrial proofer and rotary oven guarantee perfect results.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 18.0kg (100%), Water: 9.9kg (55%), Sugar: 2.16kg (12%), Margarine: 1.44kg
(8%), Salt: 360g (2%), Instant Yeast: 180g (1%), Nutmeg: 54g (0.3%).
Production Process & Setup: Mix on Speed 1 for 5 mins to blend sugar completely. Move to Speed 2 for 8
mins until elastic. Divide into 500g masses, mold tightly into greased loaf pans. Proof at 32°C and 85% humidity
for 120 mins until double in size.
Master Machinery Trick: Bake in the rotary oven at 190°C for 30 mins. Inject 5 seconds of steam at the start to
prevent a thick crust, ensuring the interior stays moist, dense, and soft for days.
Product 2: Danisa Biscuits (Bakery Style Butter Cookies)
Market Strategy & Niche: High-turnover, melt-in-the-mouth luxury treats popular in Douala supermarkets and
kiosks.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 10.0kg (100%), Soft Butter/Margarine: 6.0kg (60%), Powdered Sugar: 4.0kg
(40%), Whole Eggs: 1.5kg (15%), Vanilla Extract: 100g (1%), Salt: 50g (0.5%).
Production Process & Setup: Do not use spiral hook; use paddle or mix manually in large bins. Cream butter
and sugar until fluffy. Gradual egg additions. Fold in flour smoothly until a soft paste forms. Pipe using biscuit
extruders or nozzles onto flat trays.
Master Machinery Trick: Bake at 170°C for 12-14 mins with the oven vent/damper completely OPEN. No
steam. Moving air ensures crisp edges and golden-blonde centers.

Product 3: Classic French Croissant (Laminated Pastry)
Market Strategy & Niche: The golden standard of luxury. Local bakeries sell greasy, dense imitations. Yours will
have a high-end flaky honeycomb structure.
Commercial Formulation: Flour (T45): 10.0kg (100%), Water/Milk Chilled: 5.5kg (55%), Sugar: 1.2kg (12%),
Butter for Dough: 500g (5%), Salt: 200g (2%), Yeast: 150g (1.5%), Laminating Butter: 3.5kg (35% of dough
mass).
Production Process & Setup: Mix dough at Speed 1 for 6 mins. Chill dough block at 2°C for 2 hours. Encase
the laminating butter sheet. Complete 3 single folds with 30-min chilling rests in between. Sheet to 4mm, cut
triangles, roll into shape.
Master Machinery Trick: Proof at 26°C maximum. If your proofer goes above 28°C, the laminating butter melts
out into layers, ruining the lamination. Bake at 190°C for 18 mins with light steam.
Product 4: Pain au Chocolat (Chocolate Croissant)
Market Strategy & Niche: Twin brother to the croissant, holding premium pricing margins.
Commercial Formulation: Use the exact same laminated croissant dough recipe above. Requires two
premium dark chocolate baking sticks (batons) per unit.
Production Process & Setup: Sheet laminated dough to 4mm thick. Cut into neat rectangles (8cm x 12cm).
Place one chocolate stick at the edge, roll over once, place the second stick, and roll completely shut. Place
seam side down.
Master Machinery Trick: Ensure the molder or cutting blades don't crush the raw edges; open layers must
stay visible. Proof at 26°C and bake at 190°C for 19 mins.
Product 5: Commercial Sandwich Bread (Pain de Mie)
Market Strategy & Niche: Square, uniform white bread for high-volume supply to hotels and convenience
stores.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 18.0kg (100%), Water: 10.8kg (60%), Margarine: 900g (5%), Sugar: 900g
(5%), Salt: 360g (2%), Dry Yeast: 144g (0.8%), Milk Powder: 540g (3%).
Production Process & Setup: Mix on Speed 1 for 3 mins, Speed 2 for 6 mins. Run dough through molder set
firmly to degas. Place logs into Pullman lidded square pans.
Master Machinery Trick: Slide lids shut before proofing. Let proof until dough is 1cm below the rim. Bake at
200°C for 35 mins. The lid creates flat sides and a fine crumb texture.

Product 6: Soft Burger Buns
Market Strategy & Niche: High-demand B2B supply product for fast-food chains in Yaoundé.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 15.0kg (100%), Water: 8.25kg (55%), Whole Eggs: 1.2kg (8%), Sugar: 1.5kg
(10%), Margarine: 1.5kg (10%), Salt: 300g (2%), Yeast: 150g (1%).
Production Process & Setup: Mix to full gluten windowpane. Divide into small 80g rounds. Roll perfectly
smooth. Egg wash surface and dip in sesame seeds before traying.
Master Machinery Trick: Proof at 30°C with 80% high humidity to prevent skin wrinkling. Bake at 210°C for 11
mins. Heavy steam for 3 seconds gives a glossy, soft finish.
Product 7: Premium Brioche Loaf
Market Strategy & Niche: Rich, yellow, buttery specialty bread commanding 3x baguette retail pricing.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 10.0kg (100%), Whole Eggs (Chilled): 5.0kg (50%), Sugar: 1.5kg (15%), Soft
Unsalted Butter: 4.0kg (40%), Salt: 200g (2%), Yeast: 200g (2%).
Production Process & Setup: Mix flour, eggs, sugar, yeast on Speed 1. Once dough forms, gradually drop
chunks of butter into the moving spiral bowl over 10 mins. Mix on Speed 2 until glossy.
Master Machinery Trick: Dough will be extremely soft. Chill for 1 hour to harden the butter before shaping.
Bake at 180°C for 25 mins. Brush with egg yolk post-bake for a deep shine.
Product 8: Bakery Pound Cake (Gateau Sec)
Market Strategy & Niche: The ubiquitous dry cake slices sold at every roadside kiosk and shop.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 8.0kg, Sugar: 8.0kg, Butter/Margarine: 8.0kg, Eggs: 8.0kg (Equal 1:1:1:1
ratio), Baking Powder: 160g, Vanilla Flavor: 80g.
Production Process & Setup: Cream butter and sugar inside mixer using flat paddles. Add eggs slowly. Fold
in sieved flour and baking powder on Speed 1. Pour into large greased rectangular tin molds.
Master Machinery Trick: Bake in the rotary oven at 165°C for 45-50 mins. Absolutely zero steam. Test with a
dry skewer. Slice only after cooling completely to prevent crumbling.

Product 9: Cameroon Milk Bread (Pain au Lait)
Market Strategy & Niche: Sweet, small breakfast rolls widely loved by children and families.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 15.0kg (100%), Liquid Whole Milk: 8.25kg (55%), Sugar: 1.8kg (12%), Butter:
1.2kg (8%), Salt: 300g (2%), Yeast: 150g (1%).
Production Process & Setup: Mix to a smooth state. Divide into 60g portions. Mold into small mini-baguette
torpedo shapes. Use a razor to make 4 parallel shallow horizontal cuts.
Master Machinery Trick: Proof until double in size. Bake at 200°C for 12 mins. Spray with sugar syrup
immediately when rolling out of the oven for a sticky, shiny sweet crust.
Product 10: Fruit & Raisin Slices (Pain aux Raisins)
Market Strategy & Niche: Spiral-shaped pastry layered with sweet pastry cream and local dried raisins.
Commercial Formulation: Use Croissant Dough recipe block (10kg scale). Pastry Cream: 5.0kg, Rum-soaked
Raisins: 2.0kg.
Production Process & Setup: Sheet dough to 4mm in a large sheet. Spread a thick layer of vanilla pastry
cream across it. Sprinkle raisins uniformly. Roll the sheet into a long tight log. Slice into 2cm wheels.
Master Machinery Trick: Lay flat on baking screens. Proof for 60 mins. Bake at 190°C for 15 mins. The pastry
cream caramelizes on the tray, giving an intensely sweet base.
Product 11: Sweet Sugar Doughnuts (Beignets Boulanger)
Market Strategy & Niche: Fluffy baked bakery doughnuts rolled in white granulated sugar.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 12.0kg (100%), Water/Milk: 6.6kg (55%), Sugar: 1.2kg (10%), Butter: 960g
(8%), Salt: 240g (2%), Yeast: 144g (1.2%).
Production Process & Setup: Mix into standard bun dough. Sheet out to 2cm thickness. Stamp out rings with
a doughnut cutter. Proof on baking screens for 50 mins.
Master Machinery Trick: To save oil and maximize throughput, bake them in the rotary oven at 180°C for 12
mins instead of deep-frying. Brush with melted butter and roll in sugar immediately.

Product 12: Whole Wheat Baguette (Pain Complet)
Market Strategy & Niche: Targeting health-conscious high-income consumers in bastions like Bastos
(Yaoundé) and Bonapriso (Douala).
Commercial Formulation: Whole Wheat Flour: 12.0kg (66%), White Flour: 6.0kg (34%), Water: 12.24kg
(68%), Salt: 360g (2%), Yeast: 90g (0.5%).
Production Process & Setup: Whole wheat absorbs water slowly. Do a 20-minute autolyse (water + flour
only). Mix on Speed 2 for 4 mins max. Shape gently through the molder with looser settings.
Master Machinery Trick: Requires higher hydration (68%) due to bran absorption. Bake at 220°C for 24 mins
with heavy initial steam. Crumb will be beautifully rustic and nutty.
Product 13: Commercial Hard Dough Bread
Market Strategy & Niche: Dense, heavy crusty bread preferred by workers for long-lasting satiety.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 18.0kg (100%), Water: 9.36kg (52% low hydration), Salt: 360g (2%), Yeast:
72g (0.4%).
Production Process & Setup: Dough will be incredibly stiff. Your spiral mixer handles it, but mix on Speed 1
longer to prevent motor stress. Run multiple times through molder to fully compress.
Master Machinery Trick: Bake at 230°C for 30 mins with no steam and dampers open. This drives out all
moisture from the skin, generating a classic hard, thick, crunchy crust.
Product 14: Local Style Butter Bread (Pain Beurre)
Market Strategy & Niche: A soft yellow sandwich variant with a distinct buttery aroma filling the room.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 15.0kg (100%), Water: 8.25kg (55%), Margarine (Butter-flavored): 1.8kg
(12%), Sugar: 1.2kg (8%), Salt: 300g (2%), Yeast: 150g (1%).
Production Process & Setup: Mix thoroughly until grease completely integrates into the flour's gluten network.
Shape into 400g cylinders, bake in open top long loaf pans.
Master Machinery Trick: Add 0.1% yellow food coloring or beta-carotene to simulate high egg-butter density.
Bake at 195°C for 25 mins.

Product 15: Chocolate Sponge Sheet Roll (Swiss Roll)
Market Strategy & Niche: High efficiency baking using flat aluminum trays on your 16-tray rack.
Commercial Formulation: Eggs: 5.0kg, Sugar: 2.5kg, Flour: 2.0kg, Cocoa Powder: 500g, Baking Powder:
50g.
Production Process & Setup: Whip eggs and sugar to maximum volume. Gently fold sieved flour and cocoa.
Spread 1cm flat across 16 baking sheets lined with parchment paper.
Master Machinery Trick: Bake ultra-fast at 210°C for 7 mins flat. If over-baked, it cracks when rolling. Roll
while hot inside paper, let cool, unroll, fill with chocolate fudge, and re-roll.
Product 16: Bakery Dinner Rolls (Petits Pains)
Market Strategy & Niche: Sold in packs of 10 to restaurants, caterers, and family tables.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 18.0kg (100%), Water: 10.26kg (57%), Sugar: 900g (5%), Margarine: 900g
(5%), Salt: 360g (2%), Yeast: 180g (1%).
Production Process & Setup: Standard bun mixing. Divide into small 40g round pieces. Place 40 pieces per
large flat tray sheet in a grid layout.
Master Machinery Trick: Place them close together (1cm apart) on the tray. As they proof and bake, they will
merge into each other. Consumers pull them apart, keeping edges incredibly soft.
Product 17: Premium Savory Sausage Rolls
Market Strategy & Niche: High-margin snack item targeting students and busy office workers during lunch
peaks.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 10.0kg, Shortening/Margarine: 5.0kg, Cold Water: 4.5kg, Salt: 150g. Filling:
6.0kg seasoned local beef/chicken sausage meat fill.
Production Process & Setup: Mix a flaky shortcrust pastry dough (don't over-mix, keep fat pieces visible). Roll
out to 3mm sheets. Pipe a line of sausage filling, roll dough over, seal with egg wash, cut into 10cm pieces.
Master Machinery Trick: Score the top with 3 diagonal knife cuts. Bake at 200°C for 22 mins with the air vent
open to maximize pastry flakiness.

Product 18: Sweet Coco Bread (Pain au Coco)
Market Strategy & Niche: Specialty bread infused with coconut flakes, highly popular along coastal markets.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 15.0kg (100%), Coconut Milk/Water Mix: 8.25kg (55%), Sugar: 1.8kg (12%),
Desiccated Coconut Flakes: 1.5kg (10%), Margarine: 1.2kg (8%), Yeast: 150g (1%).
Production Process & Setup: Mix coconut flakes directly into the flour before adding liquid. Proceed with
standard sweet bread mixing. Shape into rounded split rolls.
Master Machinery Trick: Top the rolls with extra sweetened coconut flakes right before proofing. The rotary
oven roasts the flakes into a crunchy, toasted, aromatic topping.
Product 19: Classic French Batard
Market Strategy & Niche: Shorter, thicker version of the baguette; holds moisture longer and commands artisan
pricing.
Commercial Formulation: Use the exact Master Baguette formula (18kg flour scale) with identical ice split.
Production Process & Setup: Adjust molder settings to produce a short, thick log (30cm length) instead of a
long baguette. Score with one single deep vertical slash down the center.
Master Machinery Trick: Bake at 220°C for 25 mins with heavy initial steam. The single slash opens into a
wide, beautiful rustic 'ear' that local ovens can never duplicate.
Product 20: Commercial Garlic & Herb Focaccia Sheets
Market Strategy & Niche: High margin luxury savory sheets sold by weight or square slices.
Commercial Formulation: Flour: 15.0kg (100%), Water: 10.5kg (70% high hydration), Olive Oil/Vegetable Oil:
1.5kg (10%), Salt: 450g (3%), Yeast: 105g (0.7%), Garlic powder/Oregano blend.
Production Process & Setup: High hydration mix on Speed 2 for 8 mins until smooth. Pour directly onto
heavily oiled deep rectangular industrial trays. Spread with fingers.
Master Machinery Trick: Dimple the dough deeply with your fingers. Dimples trap pools of garlic oil. Proof for
45 mins. Bake at 230°C for 20 mins. Cut into lucrative squares post-bake.
