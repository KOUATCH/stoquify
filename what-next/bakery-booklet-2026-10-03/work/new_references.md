## Preservation and process references checked for this edition

N01 — [FDA: What You Need to Know About Juice Safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-know-about-juice-safety). Untreated juice hazards and whole-fruit hygiene.

N02 — [FDA: Juice HACCP Regulator Training](https://www.fda.gov/food/hazard-analysis-critical-control-point-haccp/juice-haccp-regulator-training) and [Juice HACCP questions and answers](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/guidance-industry-questions-and-answers-juice-haccp-regulation). Product-specific pathogen reduction; United States framework used as a technical reference only.

N03 — [National Center for Home Food Preservation: Freezing](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/freeze/) and [Freezing citrus fruit and juice](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/freeze/fruits/freezing-citrus-fruits/). Freezing principles and packaging; domestic guidance is not a commercial shelf-life validation.

N04 — [University of Guelph: Pasteurization of Mix](https://books.lib.uoguelph.ca/icecreamtechnologyebook/chapter/pasteurization-of-mix/). Batch ice-cream mix treatment reference, 69°C for 30 minutes; actual equipment/process qualification required.

N05 — [Yogourmet: Original Yogurt Starter](https://yogourmet.com/en/usa/product-details/original-yogurt-starter/). Current supplier instructions; verify the product and sachet supplied locally.

N06 — [Yogurt Made Simple, Washington State University Extension](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/sites/extd8/files/documents/8836/fs173emakingyogurt.pdf). Milk treatment, culture and fermentation principles; home-use holding periods are not transferred to this commercial booklet.

N07 — [Oregon State University Extension: Yogurt](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/newsletter/food-hero-monthly/yogurt-june-2020). Covered refrigeration, mould disposal and texture changes after freezing.

N08 — [UK government: Cooking safely in your business](https://www.gov.uk/food-hygiene-businesses/cooking). Cooking/reheating reference of 75°C for 30 seconds and hot holding at 63°C. These are house operational choices, not asserted Cameroonian statutory limits.

N09 — [FDA: Refrigerator Thermometers—Cold Facts about Food Safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/refrigerator-thermometers-cold-facts-about-food-safety). Refrigeration, freezing and time outside cold storage.
