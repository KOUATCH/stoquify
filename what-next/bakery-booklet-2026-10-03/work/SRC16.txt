1. Premium Authentic French Baguette (Par-Bake Baseline)
Industrial Formulation (18kg Base): Flour: 18.00 kg (100%) | Chilled Water: 10.38 kg (57.6%) | Crushed Ice:
1.50 kg (8.4%) | Salt: 360 g (2.0%) | Instant Yeast: 90 g (0.5%)
Mixing & Mechanical Processing: Target final dough temperature 23°C. Mix 3m Speed 1, 10m autolyse, 5m
Speed 2. Bulk ferment 45m. Divide at 300g, rest 15m. Run through molder with medium roller gap. Proof for
60m at 26°C, 75% humidity.
Baking, Dampening & Mechanical Controls: Preheat oven to 240°C. Score with 5 overlapping diagonal
incisions. Inject heavy steam for 15 seconds. For Par-Baking (logistics advantage): Bake for 15 mins at 220°C
until structurally set but completely pale white. Transport to POS in sealed crates, finish-bake at POS for 4 mins
at 240°C for explosive, crackling freshness.
2. Premium Kumba Bread (Local Dense Block Leader)
Industrial Formulation (18kg Base): Flour: 18.00 kg (100%) | Water: 9.00 kg (50%) | Sugar: 2.16 kg (12%) |
Margarine/Fat: 1.08 kg (6%) | Salt: 270 g (1.5%) | Instant Yeast: 180 g (1.0%) | Nutmeg: 36 g (0.2%)
Mixing & Mechanical Processing: Mix 5m Speed 1, 6m Speed 2. Dough must be dense and uniform. Bulk
ferment 30m. Divide into heavy 600g blocks. Pass through molder with tight rollers to degas completely and
create an ultra-tight, fine crumb matrix. Proof 75m in high humidity.
Baking, Dampening & Mechanical Controls: Bake at 190°C (374°F) for 35 minutes in the rotary oven. Keep
the damper completely closed for the first 25 minutes to trap internal moisture, ensuring a soft, shreddable
block structure that local wood ovens cannot match uniformly.

Section 4: The Nkambe Remote Logistics & Dust-Shield
Protocol
Operating a production plant 2 to 3 kilometers away from your primary Point of Sale (POS) in Nkambe
introduces significant risks of red road dust contamination and rapid product cooling. Your heavy-duty tricycle
delivery configuration must follow these strict operational rules:
 The Dual Airtight Container Shield: All finished staples (Kumba Bread, Sandwich Loaves, Biscuits) must
 be packed inside heavy-duty plastic transit boxes lined with clean linen sheets. Lids must be clamped
airtight before the tricycle departs to completely block dust intake.
 The Par-Bake Baguette Chain: Baguettes must follow Recipe 1's par-bake protocol. Never ship 100% fully
 baked baguettes across 3km. The air movement during tricycle transit dries out the crust and turns it
rubbery. Shipping pale, 85% baked shells locks in interior moisture.
 The POS Flash-Firing Chamber: Your retail storefront must house a small finishing oven. When the tricycle
 arrives with the dust-free, pale par-baked baguettes, flash-bake them on-demand for 3-4 minutes at 240°C.
This delivers an explosive crackle and piping hot center directly to the consumer at purchase.
 Tricycle Suspension & Stacking Laws: Dense items like Kumba Bread must occupy the bottom layer of
 the boxes. Delicate items like Croissants and Brioche must travel in separate, shallow plastic trays nested at
the top of the container to prevent crushing over rough dirt roads.

Section 6: Competitive Warfare Against Local
Wood-Fired Competitors
Traditional firewood bakeries in Nkambe survive because they offer cheap volume. However, they suffer from
uneven baking temperatures, burnt crust bottoms, and erratic weights. You will win using your industrial
consistency:
 Capturing the 'Pain-Frites' Wholesale Market: Local bread splits apart and collapses under the weight of
 greasy hot chips and mayonnaise. Take samples of your machine-molded, uniform baguettes to local street
food vendors. Show them that your strong crust holds more fries without tearing and absorbs sauces without
turning soggy. Offer a reliable daily wholesale delivery time via your tricycle line.
 The Premium Clean-Oil Pain-Frites Station: Establish your own gourmet 'Pain-Frites' hub at your main
 POS. Use your symmetrical baguettes and fry potatoes in transparent, frequently filtered vegetable oil (a
major contrast to the dark, reused oil of street vendors). Offer high-value add-ons like grilled eggs, avocado
slices, or spiced beef skewers.
 The Weight and Price Guarantee: Firewood ovens produce loaves that vary by up to 50g from batch to
 batch. Mount a digital scale at your checkout counter. Proudly display a sign stating: 'Every Baguette is
Guaranteed Machine-Molded to 250g.' This transparency immediately builds consumer trust and discredits
the competition.
