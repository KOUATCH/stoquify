3. Core High-Profitability Industrial Formulations
All formulations are scaled for an 18.00 kg commercial flour loading, optimized for mechanical molding and
16-tray rotary convective baking.
Product 1: Authentic French Baguette (Par-Bake Optimized)
Formula Matrix: Flour: 18.00 kg (100%) | Chilled Water: 10.38 kg (57.6%) | Shattered Ice Sheets: 1.50 kg
(8.4%) | Salt: 360 g (2.0%) | Instant Yeast: 90 g (0.5%)
Industrial Process: Mix 3m low, 10m autolyse, 5m high. Target temp: 23°C. Bulk ferment 45m. Scale to 300g,
mold gently. Proof 60m at 26°C. Bake 15m at 230°C with 15s steam. Cool and pack pale for POS finishing.
Product 2: Premium Kumba Bread (Local Block Staple)
Formula Matrix: Flour: 18.00 kg (100%) | Water: 9.72 kg (54%) | Margarine/Fat: 1.08 kg (6%) | Sugar: 2.16 kg
(12%) | Salt: 360 g (2%) | Instant Yeast: 180 g (1%)
Industrial Process: Mix 5m low, 6m high. Molder setting: Tight rollers to achieve a dense, tight crumb
structure. Proof 90m. Bake at 200°C for 35m in rotary oven. Open dampener during last 5m for rich color.
Product 3: Danisa-Style Premium Butter Biscuits
Formula Matrix: Low-Protein Flour: 18.00 kg (100%) | Pure Butter: 9.00 kg (50%) | Refined Sugar: 6.30 kg
(35%) | Whole Eggs: 1.80 kg (10%) | Vanilla Extract: 90 g
Industrial Process: Cream butter and sugar on Speed 1 for 4m. Add eggs gradually. Fold in flour manually or
1m on Speed 1 to avoid gluten activation. Pipe onto dry screens. Bake at 170°C for 14m. Zero steam.
Product 4: Authentic French Croissants
Formula Matrix: Flour: 18.00 kg (100%) | Cold Water: 9.90 kg (55%) | Sugar: 1.98 kg (11%) | Soft Butter
(Dough): 720 g (4%) | Roll-In Butter Sheets: 5.40 kg (30%) | Yeast: 270 g
Industrial Process: Mix dough to partial development. Cold ferment 12 hours. Encase roll-in butter sheets,
execute 1 double turn and 2 single turns. Sheet to 4mm, cut triangles, roll tight. Proof strictly at 25°C max
(prevent butter melt). Bake at 190°C for 18m with 5s steam.

To maximize afternoon retail profitability when bread sales slow down, deploy high-margin cold products

5. Nkambe Logistical Chain & Dust Shield Blueprint
Operating a 2 to 3 km transport chain using a tricycle means your product faces immediate quality deterioration
from cold wind and red road dust. Implement these strict handling protocols:
1. The Airtight Desiccator Pack: Never transport bread in open trays. All baguettes and staples must move
 inside food-grade, heavy-duty plastic boxes fitted with rubber-gasket lock lids.
2. Thermal Pre-Heating: Before stacking par-baked loaves, line the base of the plastic transport boxes with
 thick linen cloths heated briefly inside the rotary oven. This prevents internal condensation.
3. The Retail Flash-Bake: Receive par-baked baguettes at your central POS. Store inside the closed boxes.
 Upon customer purchase, place the loaf inside your small storefront oven at 240°C for 3 minutes to activate
the final crispy crust right in front of the buyer.
