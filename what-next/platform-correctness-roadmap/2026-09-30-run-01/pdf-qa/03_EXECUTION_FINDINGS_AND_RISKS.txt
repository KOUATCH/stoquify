Verification and evidence risk
Initial audit: 3,173 passed / 23 failed assertions across 326 suites, using an audit-specific adapter. Later
overlapping rechecks: people 552 passed / 15 failed; finance 677 / 3; supplement 112 / 0; changed UI 8 / 0. Thus
18 failed assertions remained in the last affected-cohort rechecks; these results must not be added to the
initial totals. Eight structural gates passed and two were blocked. Typecheck timed out after 420 seconds; the
browser could not connect. None of those commands was rerun as part of this roadmap publication.
Normal Jest and an audit adapter are different evidence classes. Mocked unit tests cannot establish PostgreSQL
lock behavior; read-only draft-data arithmetic cannot prove payment, reconciliation or payroll finalization.
Passing text scanners cannot prove runtime safety. Table/column-name parity cannot establish full
schema/privilege parity. A locally restored database cannot prove deployed disaster recovery. Country pack
PUBLISHED cannot establish every tax/payroll/fiscal capability is qualified.
