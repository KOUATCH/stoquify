# HR-14 operation and recovery

Supported ordinary decisions: lifecycle review/reject/apply; contract activation; contract-document evidence; compensation assignment; salary review/reject/apply; payment destination review/reject/apply; time-policy approval; schedule approval; time-request review/reject. Existing native owners retain their domain controls. No second approval table or engine was introduced.

Review evidence rules: displayed facts, explicit acknowledgement and 8–500 character reason; inbox approvals/application require at least one eligible retained request/approval/scan reference, and selected references must exist in the current projection. Time review requires its retained request/policy reference. Rejection still requires acknowledgement and reason. The server generates versioned canonical proof and stores its decision envelope/receipt atomically in existing audit storage. References identify retained source metadata; HR-15 delivers original document transport. They are not qualified legal approval or proof that a person opened an original file.

Current permissions remain mandatory on preview, decision and recovery. HR management alone does not grant salary visibility. Payment details are masked. Setup reviewers retain bounded employee/domain/expiry grants. A revoked, disabled or out-of-scope actor cannot recover a receipt through this entry point. Do not add aggregate permissions to the client dispatcher.

Stale source: reload current facts, inspect changes, select evidence and acknowledge again. The reason draft remains. Uncertain outcome: preserve the exact submitted envelope and use Recover decision receipt. The UI freezes editable decision fields during uncertainty. Retained receipt is authoritative even if cache invalidation fails. Queue refresh occurs after receipt dismissal. Future lifecycle application reports SCHEDULED; a later due-date preview has a new version and may apply through the native owner.

Lock order: policy decisions take organization UPDATE before account SHARE/source admission; employee decisions take live authority and employee advisory/row locks, then source locks. Native contract creation also retains its organization contract advisory lock before employee. Metadata attachment participates. Payroll/employee owners require Read Committed for operational serialization. Native business rules and immutable period boundaries still apply.

Rollback: disable the affected controlled entry point and retain receipts/audit history. Do not restore manual arbitrary-hash approval as a repair. Existing native compatibility APIs remain available to existing trusted workflows; the new controlled UI is not a retroactive rewrite of historical proofs. No migration or generated-client change was required in HR-14.

Verification environment: PostgreSQL17 at explicit owned loopback port55464, synthetic employers only, no application .env. Browser uses actual React/Radix/CSS/native services/DB and synthetic HTTP identity transport. Full authenticated Next routing, live provider behavior, actual screen-reader output, load testing and production operation were not run. Browser verifies focus trapping/return, accessible names/alert/status semantics and EN/FR reflow. Manual assistive-technology certification remains a release activity.

No live document provider, retention law, payroll rates, bank action, production schema promotion, seed, deployment or pilot activation occurs. HR-15 requires named test storage/scanning and employer-approved retention before live handling. Existing broader policy configuration and manual evidence references outside the nine approval domains retain their prior scope.
