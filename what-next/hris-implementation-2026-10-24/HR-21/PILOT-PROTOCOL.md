## Operator execution cards

Record operator, timestamp, route, source identifiers, expected/actual result, evidence path, elapsed time and recovery outcome for every card. Use synthetic data first on an inspected isolated target, then only separately authorized pilot records. A developer/database fix during an operator card fails the “without intervention” criterion until rerun through the supported UI.

1. **Setup and complete discovery.** HR opens People setup/import, selects reviewed employer policies, imports a bounded population and resolves duplicate/invalid rows. In the synthetic scale lane, search the first, middle and200th employee. In the named pilot, use its actual authorized population of1–200; counts must match those rows. Employee/manager accounts cannot discover unrelated people or salary values.
2. **Starter.** Create the employee draft through the supported authoring flow, prepare its employment/contract activation request. Complete the required contract, document and payment-destination approvals in card3 before activating employment; then independently review/apply activation and complete starter responsibilities with evidence. Confirm effective dates and current employment projection. A draft or missing approval must not become payroll-ready.
3. **Contract and compensation.** Submit contract evidence through the admitted document path. Independently approve/apply contract and compensation changes. Assign distinct, separately authenticated existing-tenant payment-destination requester, reviewer and applier; no person may collapse those three roles for that change. Check signed-document and destination evidence. Attempt issuer self-approval and cross-tenant references: both fail. Historical facts remain unchanged.
4. **Office team.** Assign a reviewed schedule, import raw attendance, request leave, approve independently, post/reconcile leave usage and resolve an attendance correction. Unresolved anomalies, pending cancellation and incomplete approval block certification. Certify exact-period inputs using the native owner.
5. **Shift team.** Repeat with an overnight shift crossing midnight and a period boundary in the employer's declared timezone. All minutes belong to the local start date. Review breaks, absence/overtime and holiday/leave treatment against the employer's approved policy; do not invent country defaults. Exercise a stale schedule/source change and recertification.
6. **Daily work.** HR/manager opens the daily queue, filters source items, resolves an exact item including one beyond a source's default page, and confirms the next refresh/counts reconcile. Unauthorized categories and employee details remain absent. Lost access during refresh hides prior content.
7. **Handoff.** Select the exact period/country, verify all included employees/exclusions/units/totals, resolve blockers in native source workflows, issue immutable originals and reconcile them as the named independent recipient. Reject one specific row with a reason, correct and independently recertify the source, issue a linked replacement and accept its exact eligible-row partition (200 in the synthetic scale lane; the actual eligible count for the named pilot). The prior bytes/receipt stay unchanged. Export/receipt never means paid or filed.
8. **Employee service and documents.** Employee retrieves only permitted own originals, submits a correction/case and follows a return-for-information cycle. Manager/HR act only within current scope. Confirm scanner rejection, download failure, expired/held evidence and role revocation through the qualified provider path. Synthetic storage tests do not satisfy provider admission.
9. **Leaver.** Schedule and independently approve termination, complete assigned asset/access responsibilities with evidence, and verify effective-period inclusion/exclusion. Manual task attestation does not automatically revoke external accounts. External revocation needs its own authorized operator/provider evidence.
10. **Failure and recovery.** Exercise unknown mutation response versus confirmed write with failed refresh; exact retry recovers one outcome. Verify EN/FR,360/768/1440 widths, keyboard focus/labels, unavailable states and actual session/module revocation. Execute the separately reviewed restore procedure and reconcile retained IDs/bytes/counts before resuming work.

## Measurement and decision

Measure actual time to import/onboard, resolve attendance, close inputs, reconcile a replacement and answer an employee request. Record hardware/data volume/network and operator interventions. Synthetic timing is an observation, not a production SLA or claimed efficiency improvement.

HR operator, independent reviewer, support owner and accountable launch owner review exceptions together. Any unresolved critical correctness/access failure, unqualified provider, unknown migration/restore result or missing required owner prevents launch. A narrow named pilot needs a recorded decision before broader rollout. LIVE_PAYROLL requires its separate HR20A–D country evidence and a renewed decision.
