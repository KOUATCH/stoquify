## Quality Gates Added

The suite blocks 9+ status unless these conditions are met:

- First-time visitors can identify the category in 5-10 seconds.
- CTAs match the real sales motion.
- Public-to-auth transitions do not create confusing dead ends.
- Every public proof claim has owner, source file, approval status, locale coverage, and review/expiry date.
- Public visuals are classified as real, redacted, sample, demo, or mockup.
- Sample/demo data is visibly labeled.
- Unsupported wording such as certified, guaranteed, legally compliant, or tax-approved is blocked unless evidence and approval exist.
- EN/FR copy preserves meaning and text fit.
- WCAG 2.2 AA review has zero Critical/Serious issues before release signoff.
- Keyboard, reduced-motion, responsive screenshot, production build, and performance gates pass or have documented remediation.
- Analytics events contain no personal, tenant, customer, payroll, payment, receipt, or financial payloads.
