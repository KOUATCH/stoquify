# AF16 prepared pilot protocol

25 October 2026. **PILOT_PREPARED; live execution deferred.** This document is a proposal for a later authorized pilot, not evidence of recruited firms, observed benefit or paid retention.

## Participants and scope

Propose three accounting firms with five to ten existing clients each, spanning two monthly close cycles. A firm manager owns allocation; a preparer handles requests and preparation; an independent reviewer approves support; a client owner responds and agrees advisory delivery. Use existing identities, named mandates, managed engagements and current module permissions. Assignment alone grants no client access.

The development expansion covers AF01–AF13. Before recruitment, choose the exact live cut. AF11 statutory candidates stay UNKNOWN without qualified country evidence. AF14 and AF15 are excluded. AF13 captures manually declared effort and separate-currency planning budgets; it does not calculate payroll, invoices, profitability, utilization or realized revenue.

## Baseline and measurement definitions

Record the client, engagement, accounting period, observation window, source and missingness for every metric. The accountable firm manager verifies cohort inclusion. Do not compare an incomplete period with a complete period or substitute zero for missing values.

| Measure | Definition and source | Baseline collection |
|---|---|---|
| Request touches | Retained evidence requests and their attributable follow-up activity; exclude refreshes and page visits | One prior comparable monthly cycle, from dated firm records; mark unavailable when unsupported |
| Client waiting | Request timestamp to first relevant client response, within the selected cohort/window | Retained request/response timestamps; unfinished intervals remain open |
| Review delay | Submitted response to independent acceptance timestamp | Workflow receipts; acceptance does not imply financial signoff |
| Rework | Retained replacement/correction requests and reasons | Review receipts and manually confirmed prior-cycle records |
| Close elapsed time | Defined start-of-close to retained completion event | Agree start and completion definitions before observing either cycle; incomplete closes remain censored |
| Active work | Manually declared effort, distinguishing corrected and independently reviewed declarations | Staff consent and explicit manual declarations; session duration is not work time |
| Staffing decisions | Documented allocation decision, inputs considered, reason and later outcome | Manager decision diary tied to engagement/period and AF13 records |
| Meaningful repeat work | Distinct days with an attributable request, response, review, approved export, advisory or allocation action | Existing retained business receipts; do not count background refreshes or force extra visits |
| Adoption and retention | Eligible actors completing useful work and firms choosing to continue after two cycles | Structured interviews and a dated continuation decision; payment/paid retention requires actual evidence |

## Two-cycle procedure

1. Before activation, name owners, select exact scope/country coverage, agree latency/error budgets, verify support contacts and obtain the separate authorization for real enrollment. Capture baseline availability and sample exclusions.
2. For cycle one, use the smallest agreed firm/client cohort. Observe blocked, denied, stale and uncertain outcomes, retries, queue age, support requests and duplicate effects. Review permission boundaries with each firm manager.
3. At the first review, compare like-for-like completed intervals, record denominator/missingness, and separate product defects from training or unavailable source evidence. Change no success targets retrospectively.
4. In cycle two, repeat with the same core cohort where feasible; report attrition and changes. Assess whether staffing decisions and reduced waiting/rework are supported by retained evidence and interviews.
5. Produce a decision with cohort counts, distributions, exceptions, qualitative evidence and limitations. Claim improvement or willingness to pay only when observed. Keep broader rollout separate.

## Owners and decisions still required

- Product lead: recruit only after explicit authorization; select cohorts and agree success criteria before measurement.
- Firm manager and accounting reviewer: confirm definitions, baseline validity, independent review and usefulness.
- Engineering and operations: technical gates, performance budgets, incident response and restrictive rollback.
- Qualified country reviewer: official definitions, effective dates and AF11 support claims.

No messages, recruitment, real enrollment, billing, provider connection or production activation were performed by this development verification.
