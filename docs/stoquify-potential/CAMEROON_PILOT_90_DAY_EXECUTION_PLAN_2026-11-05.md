# Cameroon Pilot 90 Day Execution Plan

**Prepared:** 5 November 2026  
**Basis:** Stoquify Potential Assessment dated 2 November 2026  
**Sponsor:** Founder  
**Market:** Stockholding SMEs in one Cameroon city  
**Planning envelope:** $150,000 maximum across three conditional tranches; $850,000 retained from the stated $1 million budget  
**Schedule:** 90 calendar days from an approved kickoff; Weeks 1–12 cover Days 1–84 and Week 13 covers Days 85–90

## 1 The decision this pilot must support

The pilot will determine whether one repeatable Stoquify offer can help a defined group of Cameroon SMEs reconcile sales, stock, money received and customer balances, while producing usable records for their accountants. Success requires paid use, trustworthy records, measurable benefit and delivery economics that can support a business.

The immediate next step is a two-week discovery sprint in one reachable city. Name the pilot and field leads, observe about ten businesses across hardware/electrical wholesale and FMCG distribution, and compare their actual workflows. Finish the larger discovery sample and select one segment by Day 21.

The founder has confirmed access to businesses in Cameroon. The city, named team members, supplier rates, customer commitments and actual availability of funds remain to be established. Assign accountable individuals by Day 3. Roles in this plan are proposed assignments, not claims that those people have been hired.

Day 1 begins when the founder records the kickoff, named owners, city, capacity and initial budget ceiling. Maintain an actual calendar beside the relative schedule, including each firm's trading days, planned closures and staff availability. Shift dates when kickoff changes; retain the gates and observation requirements.

This document prepares future work. It does not enroll customers, spend money, deploy software or change existing production decisions. Current release evidence and any explicit deferral of live validation remain effective until their required authorities record a changed decision. The status register inspected on 5 November retains production NO_GO and country qualification gaps [R2].

### Day 90 target

- One city, one selected SME segment and one defined commercial offer.
- Eight intended pilot SMEs tracked as a single cohort, with no hidden replacement of unsuccessful firms.
- Six migration cases drawn from those eight SMEs, rather than six additional customers.
- Three candidate accountant partners, with separate evidence for their usefulness as users and as a sales channel.
- At least six of eight firms completing the core workflow on at least 80% of their eligible trading days.
- At least 95% independently checked event capture for each firm, with zero unresolved platform-caused loss or duplication of economic effects.
- At least 30% median reduction in comparable active reconciliation work across six usable paired firm observations, including Stoquify's operational support effort.
- At least five of the original eight firms paying the agreed first subscription period and its first renewal, with mature observation windows.
- A documented proceed, revise, extend or stop decision supported by costs, incidents, missing evidence and customer outcomes.

These are proposed decision thresholds. They do not establish statistical market fit, durable retention, lifetime value or readiness for additional countries.

## 2 Pilot scope and customer selection

### Choose one segment from observed evidence

Compare hardware/electrical-supply wholesalers with FMCG distributors. Complete 18–24 unique SME discovery records, seeking roughly balanced coverage before selection, and interview 4–6 accountants or accounting firms. Interview the owner and at least one frontline user where possible. Count an SME once even if several people participate.

Observe receiving, units and pricing, sales, returns, credit sales, collections, cash and payment matching, physical stock checks and day-end reconciliation. Record what was observed separately from what was reported. Ask to examine a recent real discrepancy or delayed task with permission. Establish its frequency, active work, business consequence, current solution and decision maker.

Use a simple 0–2 rating for each of six criteria: recurring material pain; owner willingness to change; fit with the selected workflow; data and opening-position feasibility; reachable staff and support access; and willingness to accept explicit paid terms. Scores support judgment and do not override a failed mandatory requirement. Retain rejected prospects and reasons so the team can see whether product limits exclude the commercially attractive market.

Select one city based on actual weekly field access and one segment by Day 21. If neither segment shows repeatable pain and a plausible paid offer, revise the segment before committing to broad engineering work.

### Cohort design

Publish a proposed roster of eight firms by Day 28. Aim for four directly recruited firms and four distinct firms referred by at least two accountants. This is an indicative channel comparison, not a statistically powered experiment. Keep a separate reserve list and log every change from the Day 28 proposal. Finalize and lock the eight headline cohort IDs and channel attribution before the first live activation. After that lock, substitutions cannot improve the headline denominator: non-activations and dropouts remain in the original eight-firm results, and no reserve firm replaces them in the success calculation.

Each candidate needs an accountable owner, an available operator, agreed trading calendar, an independent record source, appropriate authority and data arrangements, and willingness to complete baseline observation and discuss paid terms. Qualify its catalog, tax/receipt needs, returns, credit workflow, payment channels, opening data and connectivity against actual supported scope.

The inspected opening preview is restricted to CM/XAF and bounded master-data selections; opening application requires a financially empty client [R5]. Screen these conditions explicitly. Do not choose artificially simple firms and then infer that large or complex distributors are served. If most attractive prospects exceed current bounds, produce a scoped investment decision or change the target segment.

### Offer to test

Use one clearly described core offer: daily reconciliation of in-scope sales, stock, collections and customer balances, an owner attention list and an accountant handoff. Quote a separate, limited setup service and optional professional assistance. State which organization, locations, users, data imports, support hours and deliverables are included.

By Day 28, choose and record a recurring XAF price to test, setup fee, billing start, 30-calendar-day term, renewal due date, payment evidence, discount/refund treatment and support allowance. The assessment's 25,000 XAF/month example is an arithmetic illustration, not a validated selling price. Keep comparable scope and terms across firms, or document package differences before outcomes are known. Research incentives must not be disguised as subscription revenue.

### Mandatory live admission checklist

- Actual tenant, role, mandate and independent-review boundaries work for owner, operator and accountant; revoked or denied authority is tested in the selected journey.
- Sales, returns, stock/value, customer balances, collection evidence and ledger/close effects reconcile for the included workflow. Retries and lost responses do not create unexplained duplicate or missing effects.
- Applicable receipt/invoice treatment is accepted by the qualified country reviewer. The present non-statutory on-screen receipt limitation is not marketed as resolved until the relevant evidence changes [R3].
- Production offline replay stays unavailable while its test-only boundary applies. Agree a practical connectivity/incumbent fallback; admission does not silently enable offline sales [R4].
- Supported payment channels and provider evidence are specified. A payment record or matching result is not proof that Stoquify moved money or has a live provider integration.
- Opening scope, approvals, balances, source version, recovery and tenant financial-empty conditions are accepted. Unsupported store-credit instruments or historical migration are excluded unless separately implemented and qualified.
- Actual contracts, customer authority, data location/processing, backup, access and support arrangements are reviewed for this deployment. Sensitive payroll/tax features stay outside the offer unless their own applicable requirements are met.
- Current operational evidence addresses the selected environment, artifact, monitoring, backup/restore, rollback/recovery and support coverage. Mocks, source inspection and historical runs remain separate evidence classes [R8].

The checklist makes known report constraints actionable. It does not replace the repository's release process or qualified professional judgment.

### Measurement protocol

Define eligible event types and the daily operating checklist before baseline collection. Compare the same firms over equivalent task sets; log business closures, outages, unusual promotions, volume changes and seasonal effects. Count person-minutes by participant so parallel work by different people is visible, while preventing duplicate entries for the same person's task. Keep one-time migration/training and product-development time separately visible.

Declare the normalization method before baseline collection: fixed closing person-minutes per trading day plus variable person-minutes per eligible event type, standardized to each firm's baseline median daily event counts. Freeze those reference counts and task definitions when the baseline closes, before reviewing pilot outcomes. Standardized minutes equal fixed closing minutes plus the sum of minutes per event type multiplied by its frozen reference count; classify each person's task time once.

For each paired firm, calculate reduction as (baseline standardized active minutes minus pilot standardized active minutes) divided by baseline standardized active minutes. Report the individual results and the median, alongside capture coverage. Faster processing with incomplete records cannot pass the benefit criterion. At least six usable pairs are required; disclose every unusable observation.

Freeze the D85 dataset with extraction time, covered dates, cohort IDs, versions, reviewer, missing fields and corrections. Retain both original-cohort outcomes and mature-observation subsets. A small, selected cohort gives directional learning; it cannot substantiate all-SME or regional outcome claims.

## 9 Operating playbooks

### Research and consent

FL prepares a concise discussion guide and participant information. Record the purpose, permitted access, data to be viewed, recording permission and withdrawal/contact route. Use minimum necessary records. The owner and frontline user should explain the same task independently where practical. Do not promise functionality, regulatory coverage or deployment dates that remain unqualified.

### Standard opening and onboarding

FL checks eligibility and scope; AQ reviews source mappings and opening assumptions. Preview and resolve discrepancies before approvals. Use the existing independent approval/application controls, then reconcile the resulting stock, balances and financial records. Record version, actor, date, approvals and recovery reference. Do not repeatedly apply a cutover after a lost response without checking retained results. Train owner/operator separately, complete a witnessed task, and confirm escalation and export/exit arrangements. Record all customer and staff effort.

### Daily operation

The operator records agreed in-scope activity; FL or the assigned reviewer checks independent event capture and exceptions. At day end, compare sales, cash/payment evidence, stock movements and customer-balance changes. The owner receives a short list of explained totals, unresolved differences and actions with owners. An accountant reviews the scheduled handoff. Record stale or missing evidence explicitly; avoid presenting unknown amounts as zero.

### Support and incident response

Agree staffed support hours before enrollment. Keep one customer contact route and a severity register. During staffed hours, target acknowledgment of a suspected financial/access/data incident within 30 minutes and of routine workflow requests within one business day; these are planned service targets subject to available staffing. Customers have a documented incumbent fallback for hours when the team cannot respond.

On a suspected hard-stop incident, pause the affected action, preserve evidence and identify scope; TL coordinates, QA verifies, AQ reviews financial implications and the relevant professional handles data/contract obligations. Avoid guessing that a failed response means a failed transaction. Resume only after documented resolution and required requalification. Never delete or silently repair evidence to improve the scorecard.

### Accountant review and commercial follow-up

Agree whether each firm acts as user, reviewer, implementation partner, referrer or paying buyer. Record conflicts, responsibilities and compensation. Track evidence requests, responses, corrections and professional review time. CL records offer acceptance, objections, first-term payment, cancellation/refund and renewal at the actual declared terms. FO reconciles receipts independently of sales intent.

## 11 Day 90 decision and evidence packet

PL receives a reviewed packet containing the charter and named owners; discovery/segment records; original cohort and exclusions; actual release/admission decisions; artifact and evidence references; independent openings and event checks; baseline/benefit calculations; support/incident history; offer/payment/renewal records; accountant-channel results; setup and recurring economics; and cash paid, commitments, remaining liabilities and retained reserve.

Choose one outcome explicitly:

1. **Proceed narrowly:** all mandatory live conditions remain satisfied; the declared adoption, quality, benefit, renewal and economics criteria are supported. Approve only the next defined segment/capacity phase and budget. Wider OHADA expansion requires its own country and channel evidence.
2. **Revise the offer or segment:** the pain exists but price, package, workflow fit or channel fails. Name the changed hypothesis, owner, cap and deadline before further investment.
3. **Extend observation under a new cap:** evidence is promising but insufficiently mature. Specify the missing question, required exposure, maximum additional commitment and decision date. Do not call this a successful commercial pilot.
4. **Stop or suspend live expansion:** unresolved mandatory failures, weak paid demand or persistent bespoke delivery prevents a defensible continuation. Complete customer continuity, refunds/obligations, exports and data handling through agreed procedures.

Distinguish a disproved hypothesis from missing evidence. Reviewers record dissent and qualifications. The $850,000 reserve has no automatic release; its actual amount depends on cash, commitments and any separately authorized decisions.
