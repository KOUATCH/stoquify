# Cameroon CNPS Expert Review Checklist

Complete every item and cross-reference the signed approval artifact.

## Authority and effective law

- [ ] Authenticate each relied-on official source and issuing authority.
- [ ] Confirm whether Decree No. 2016/072 remains effective for the approved 2026 window.
- [ ] Search for and assess amendments, replacements, suspensions, repeals, circulars, transition rules, and later CNPS classifications.
- [ ] Explain the effect of Article 11's rate-revision provision.
- [ ] Identify unsupported periods, categories, or claims.

## Pension

- [ ] Confirm 8.40% total, 4.20% employer, and 4.20% employee.
- [ ] Confirm XAF 750,000 monthly and XAF 9,000,000 annual ceilings.
- [ ] Review below-cap, at-cap, above-cap, minimum-base, and rounding scenarios.
- [ ] State whether annual regularization or other exceptions affect the fixtures.

## Family allowances

- [ ] Confirm general/domestic-worker, agriculture, and private-education rates.
- [ ] Confirm employer-only funding.
- [ ] Review sector classification and ambiguous-sector treatment.
- [ ] Independently calculate all supplied scenarios.

## Occupational risks

- [ ] Confirm Groups A, B, and C rates and employer-only funding.
- [ ] Review the decree annex and current classification procedure.
- [ ] Explain mixed-activity, highest-risk, inspection, and reclassification treatment.
- [ ] Independently calculate all supplied scenarios.

## Employer rules

- [ ] Confirm registration, employee declaration, calculation, withholding, payment, teledeclaration, and control obligations.
- [ ] Confirm current timing, frequency, procedural exceptions, and authority.
- [ ] Distinguish legal obligation from administrative guidance.

## Final decision

- [ ] Record one decision for every fixture family.
- [ ] Record exact provisions and effective dates.
- [ ] Produce an independent tie-out hash for every family.
- [ ] Record limitations and exclusions.
- [ ] State explicitly whether production use is approved.
- [ ] Sign the decision using an accepted method.

Accepted decisions are `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `CHANGES_REQUIRED`, and `MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`. Only `APPROVED` for all four required families may support production.
