# Optional Draft Request for CNPS Confirmation

**Do not send without authorized legal/compliance approval.**

This request is not required for the repository's `EXPERT_REVIEWED` production path. Use it only if Stoquify intends to retain or publish the stronger claim that its interpretation or implementation is `REGULATOR_CONFIRMED`.

## Draft

To: The competent authority, Caisse Nationale de Prévoyance Sociale du Cameroun  
Subject: Request for written confirmation of CNPS contribution interpretation for country-pack `CM-2026.1`

Stoquify respectfully requests written confirmation concerning the application of Decree No. 2016/072 and current CNPS employer guidance for the period beginning 1 January 2026.

The proposed implementation uses:

- pension: 8.40% total, divided 4.20% employer and 4.20% employee, with XAF 750,000 monthly and XAF 9,000,000 annual ceilings;
- family allowances: 7.00% general/domestic workers, 5.65% agriculture, and 3.70% private education, employer-funded;
- occupational risks: 1.75% Group A, 2.50% Group B, and 5.00% Group C, employer-funded;
- employer responsibility for registration, employee declaration, calculation, withholding, declaration, and payment.

Please confirm separately:

1. whether Decree No. 2016/072 remains applicable for the stated 2026 period;
2. whether the listed rates, ceilings, funding allocations, and classifications are current;
3. whether the stated employer obligations are complete for the intended scope;
4. whether the attached calculation fixtures correctly apply those provisions;
5. whether your response confirms only the applicable rules, the interpretation, the fixture calculations, or also permits a claim of regulator confirmation for production use.

Please include the official organization, named signatory and title, response reference number, date, effective period, covered fixture families, limitations, signature method, and signed response.

Attachments should include the four-family fixture summary, source inventory, and independent calculations after legal/compliance approval.
