03 / Recommended logo system

One mark. Every setting.

The master artwork is true SVG geometry. The wordmark is outlined, so the logo renders without an installed font.

App icon
Social avatar

Built from a steady line.

Rounded bends soften the mark. The angled terminals add motion. Its form remains the same across themes, modules, and printed materials.

Clear space: at least ¼ of the symbol’s visible height on all sides. Keep descriptors separate from the core logo.

Minimums: symbol 16 px; horizontal lockup 140 px; stacked lockup 120 px. For print, start at 6 mm, 35 mm, and 30 mm respectively; proof with the printer.

07 / Reproduction rules

Small details. Lasting consistency.

The system includes all-black, all-white, full-color, and reversed logos, plus stacked and wordmark-only lockups.

Keep it legible.

16
24
32
48 px

Below 140 px wide, use the symbol instead of a horizontal lockup. The favicon uses a solid teal tile.

Use color by role.

White labels on deep teal. Ink labels on mint and citron. Separate status palettes for success, warning, error, and information.

Use the stronger control-border token for inputs. Subtle dividers are decorative.

Give it room.

Keep at least ¼ symbol-height clear space. Avoid adding shadows, gradients, or outlines to the logo itself.

Decorative curved bands may echo the S. Keep them away from text and never use them as unlabeled data graphics.

✕ Do not stretch or squash.
✕ Do not lose contrast.
✕ Do not add effects.
