2.   Investment Thesis

Consistent daily production

The operating hypothesis is that controlled batch sizes and scheduled dispatch can improve product consistency. The H2 planner provides explicit clocks and resource assumptions, not measured factory output. Three acceptable independent campaigns should establish a repeatable baseline before scaling. [R1, R2]

Shared production with measured retail demand

Facility A prepares breads, fillings and other products; Facility B handles specified retail finishing and assembly. This can simplify standardization, but cooling, transport, retail staging and unsold goods remain costs and capacity constraints. The proposed programme must be matched to recorded orders and sell-through. [R1, R2]

A controlled recipe portfolio

The anthology contains 138 working recipe or component cards, with separate historical and research records. These counts are not unique commercial products or proven successes. H3 corrects two working cards and 92 historical variants affected by EDC being treated as margarine; EDC remains unidentified. [R1, R3]
