## H3: baguette production programme

The exact scheduling source was found: S038, Staggered_Baguette_Production_Schedule.pdf, in the owner's Downloads folder. The separate H3 planning module identifies seven source candidates, explains contradictions, and supplies the new BAG-H2-01 direct-dough recipe. Existing poolish and soft baguette recipes retain their own methods. The revised programme is included in Volume 1.

The earlier 505-piece claim does not allow for handling loss or the 80-piece oven-load assumption. H3 shows the original calculation, the corrected five-batch yield, and the extra loads needed. It also explains how a conditional 505-retained-piece target differs from 505 raw dough portions.

All clock times below are local bakery planning times. Preparation starts at 02:00 and the first mix at 02:20. First packing release is 06:28; default first vehicle departure is 06:33 and retail arrival 07:03. These times are editable assumptions, not measured performance. A 45-minute cooling allowance does not by itself establish packing readiness.

| Programme | Raw portions / retained illustration | Last packing release |
|---|---|---|
| Commissioning: 2 half loads, 90-minute starts | 80 / 78 | 07:58 |
| Normal: 5 full loads, 45-minute starts | 400 / 390 | 09:28 |
| Extended: 10 full loads, 45-minute starts | 800 / 780 | 13:13 |

The 2% rejection allowance is rounded upward per batch. Actual rejects, sell-through and returns must be recorded. A nominal 300 g raw portion at 15% baking loss becomes 255 g; neither loss nor finished mass is guaranteed.

At five baguettes per tray and sixteen trays per load, a full load holds 80. Confirm the layout with shaped dough, expansion and actual oven racks. The normal programme provisionally requires two full-rack proofing positions, four circulating tray/rack sets with a fifth as contingency, two cooling/packing rack positions, separate dispatch staging and a vehicle whose usable capacity is confirmed. Rack interchangeability and measured mixer minimum/maximum loads remain checks before release. Staffing reservations cover named tasks; quality monitoring, breaks and paid shift coverage must be demonstrated in a timed trial.

If proofing takes 90 minutes, two proofing positions do not sustain the default cadence. If oven recovery takes the source's alternative 23 minutes, the default cadence also fails. Slow or stop mixing when downstream capacity is unavailable; the dough endpoint controls release.

## Verification and practical limits

The staged search identified 590 filename candidates and 179 different file contents, plus relevant image screening. All candidates have a recorded disposition. Protected folders, long-path failures, cloud-only files and documents with unrelated filenames limit discovery. This is not a claim to have inspected every file on the computer.

Working-card ingredient sums, commercial scaling factors, price extensions, component references, historical revisions and the corrected cake-doughnut total were checked programmatically. Unknown prices were not converted to zero. The bread poolish allocation and handling-loss convention were also checked. Three aggregated ingredient-register columns were excluded from the recipe count because they combined multiple recipes or represented multipliers rather than ingredient masses.

PDF checks cover page count, extractable text and page-boundary overflow. Every working-card ID is present. The working volume was reviewed in page-contact overviews and selected pages at readable magnification; sample pages from the comparison and research volumes were also inspected. These checks are document-quality checks, not proof that every historical quantity or OCR numeral is correct. Small continuation pages can occur where a source record ends.

The retained research pages preserve original quantities and wording, including disputed methods. They are comparison evidence, not production instructions. Review the issue printed before each record; C01–C05 in the working booklet override conflicting food-safety advice. Scanned pastry recipes and household measures need image checks, weighing and process development before adoption.

No signed repeat-batch records were supplied. Full operating costs, sale prices, sale-through, shelf life, milk specifications and several equipment capabilities remain unresolved. All working formulas therefore require production trials; historical and research records are explicitly held where further specification is needed. This edition does not guarantee profitability or claim commercial validation of the complete source library.
