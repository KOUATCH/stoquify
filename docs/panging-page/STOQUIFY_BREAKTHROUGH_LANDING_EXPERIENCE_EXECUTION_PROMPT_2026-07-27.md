# Stoquify Breakthrough Landing Experience — Execution Prompt

**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Mode:** Research, product strategy, and implementation planning  
**Primary audience:** Early-stage founders and owner-operators  
**Product:** Stoquify  
**Required output:** Evidence-backed report with exactly three breakthrough concepts

## Mission

Study the “For Early-stage founder” experience on Accio Work and compare it with current, high-quality landing experiences from other innovative software companies. Produce exactly three structurally distinct ideas that could give Stoquify a material edge in:

1. presentation and professional look and feel;
2. clarity and strength of messaging;
3. ability to capture attention;
4. qualified conversion;
5. activation and user-retention potential.

Do not produce a generic inspiration list or visual mood board. Ground every recommendation in:

- Stoquify’s real landing-page structure and current implementation;
- existing Stoquify strategy, assurance, and UX artifacts;
- verified patterns on live official product sites;
- Stoquify’s actual differentiation: connected operations, explicit scope, source-linked evidence, controls, and OHADA/SYSCOHADA-oriented operational finance;
- a concrete, bounded implementation path.

## Required working method

### 1. Establish the real Stoquify baseline

Inspect the current landing page, its section order, its English and French messages, its CTA destinations, and its existing tests. Determine what is already implemented before proposing anything.

At minimum, inspect:

- the landing route composition;
- hero, workflow, product-gallery, operations-map, use-case, trust, adoption/pricing, and final-CTA components;
- English and French landing messages;
- browser and structural tests for the founder journey;
- recent landing-page reviews and UI/UX completion plans;
- relevant architecture graph material, while treating source code as authoritative if the graph is stale.

Explicitly identify completed work that must not be re-proposed.

### 2. Study Accio’s early-stage-founder pattern

Study the official Accio Work experience, including localized official pages if the main JavaScript surface cannot be read directly. Extract the underlying mechanics rather than copying its styling or wording.

Evaluate:

- how the page makes the visitor self-identify;
- how it turns an abstract platform into a staged journey;
- how it moves from an idea to an operating outcome;
- how the visual system communicates progress, guidance, and momentum;
- how the experience reduces the early-stage founder’s cognitive load;
- how its calls to action relate to the visitor’s current stage.

### 3. Compare with 8–12 additional official sites

Use current official pages only. Favor products that demonstrate one or more of:

- complex systems made understandable;
- product-led visual storytelling;
- role- or job-based personalization;
- strong trust and proof architecture;
- progressive disclosure;
- lifecycle or workflow narratives;
- compelling interaction without accessibility or performance excess.

Include a varied comparison set such as Stripe, Linear, Ramp, Mercury, Vercel, Notion, Shopify, Brex, Deel, Attio, and Retool. Record transferable patterns and reject patterns that would be misleading or unsuitable for Stoquify.

### 4. Produce exactly three breakthrough concepts

The three concepts must be structurally distinct. They may not be three cosmetic variants of the same hero or carousel.

For each concept, provide:

- a memorable name;
- the founder problem it solves;
- the core interaction and visitor journey;
- why it is distinctive for Stoquify;
- the source patterns that inspired it;
- a concrete desktop and mobile treatment;
- accessibility and reduced-motion behavior;
- specific changes to existing Stoquify sections;
- content and data dependencies;
- privacy, performance, evidence, and compliance boundaries;
- at least two impact dimensions;
- leading and lagging KPIs;
- a falsification condition;
- an implementation sequence;
- effort, risk, and prerequisites.

Do not claim guaranteed conversion or retention uplifts. State hypotheses and define how they would be tested.

### 5. Respect Stoquify’s current information architecture

Do not add a new top-level landing section unless it replaces or consolidates an existing one. The page is already dense.

Prefer:

- replacing, merging, or upgrading existing sections;
- progressive disclosure;
- a single qualified rollout CTA before the adoption section;
- `/register` only when the visitor reaches adoption;
- server-rendered primary content;
- lightweight enhancement rather than a heavy client-only experience;
- current design tokens and bilingual content architecture;
- real product screenshots or clearly labelled representative samples;
- explicit scope and proof rather than inflated claims.

Avoid:

- broad visual redesign;
- autoplay or scroll-jacking;
- forced horizontal carousels;
- artificial urgency;
- fabricated customer results, certifications, or regulatory coverage;
- hidden tenant-data collection;
- pre-auth personal or sensitive data;
- personalization that changes business truth;
- another feature catalogue;
- broad entitlement-enforcement work unrelated to the landing experience.

## Required report structure

1. Executive recommendation.
2. Current Stoquify baseline and completed work.
3. Accio early-stage-founder analysis.
4. Cross-site pattern library with official source links.
5. Three concepts, each fully specified.
6. Comparative scorecard.
7. Recommended portfolio and sequencing.
8. Phased implementation plan with owners, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and tests.
9. Measurement and experimentation plan.
10. Risks, guardrails, and decisions still requiring product-owner approval.
11. Source register.

## Decision framework

Score each concept from 1–5 on:

- differentiation;
- message clarity;
- visual memorability;
- professional trust;
- qualified conversion potential;
- activation and retention potential;
- mobile resilience;
- accessibility;
- delivery feasibility;
- performance safety;
- fit with Stoquify’s current architecture.

Use **5 as the favorable score** for every criterion. For delivery feasibility and performance safety, 5 means easier or safer.

Recommend:

- one lead concept;
- one supporting personalization concept;
- one trust foundation;
- the order in which they should be implemented.

## Verification requirements

Before declaring the work complete:

- verify that the report contains exactly three concepts;
- verify that each concept includes at least two impact dimensions;
- verify that all external examples link to official sources;
- verify that completed founder-journey work is recognized rather than duplicated;
- verify that the plan preserves bilingual support, keyboard access, reduced motion, mobile layout, and server-rendered meaning;
- verify that no unsupported numeric claim is presented as fact;
- verify that no broad product entitlement or authorization change is proposed;
- verify that the output PDF opens, has extractable text, and contains no placeholder content.
