# Cameroon HRIS/payroll — legal source and review register

Research date: 7 October 2026. Target confirmed by the user: private-sector SMEs with 10–250 employees. This is an engineering research register, not a signed statutory parameter release. A source being found, read or current-dated is not professional acceptance of a calculation. No payroll rates were changed.

Status vocabulary: **READ** = relevant primary-source text inspected; **INDEXED** = official indexed extracts available but direct fetch failed; **LOCATED** = official publication found, substantive text not verified; **REVIEW REQUIRED** = applicability, current amendments or interpretation still require qualified review. All sources require an amendment check through the eventual release date. A 2026 compilation alone does not prove no subsequent amendment exists.

## Concise statutory observations

**Tax (S01):** arts. 30–34 concern salary bases, exemptions and benefits; art. 34 specifies professional-expense relief and a cap. Arts. 69/71 address progressive IRPP and CAC. Arts. 81–84 cover withholding, the below-62,000 gross threshold, remittance and DIPE. C86–C88 address TDL. Annexes contain RAV and CFC/FNE instruments; the latter specifies base rounding. Legacy annex deadlines and current filing practice must be reconciled before configuring one calendar. These are review locators, not a complete approved payroll formula.

**Social contributions (S03, corroborated S01):** pension and family-benefit contribution ceilings differ from occupational-risk treatment. Decree art. 8 bases occupational risk on remuneration less professional expenses; it does not apply the art. 4 ceiling. Sector and risk classification must be evidenced. Employer guidance (S04) separates declaration, payment and supporting receipts; it describes payment within 15 days following the relevant month.

**Employment (S05):** art. 69 addresses individual payslips and payment evidence; art. 80 distinguishes agricultural and non-agricultural working-time regimes. Arts. 89–93 govern paid leave, enhancements, taking leave and related payment. Contractual/collective terms and implementing instruments matter. Obtain the payslip-format order, overtime rules, relevant collective agreements, termination/indemnity rules and deduction limits for the pilot employer; this audit does not certify those details.

**Minimum wage (S06):** the 2023 text distinguishes private agricultural and other sectors; the 2024 text addresses state workers under the Labour Code. A single unqualified national default is unsuitable. More favourable sector/contract terms remain a separate review question.

**Accounting/regional scope (S07/S10):** OHADA's official explanation establishes an accounting framework, not one common national payroll tax schedule. Treat bank/PSP formats and settlement evidence as adapters; the existence of a regional regulation does not establish that Stoquify is licensed to provide payment services.
