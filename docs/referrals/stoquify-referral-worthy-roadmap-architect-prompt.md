# Stoquify Referral-Worthy Product Roadmap Architect Prompt

```text
Act as a senior product strategy architect, SaaS execution advisor, enterprise systems designer, and growth roadmap planner.

Use the document at:

docs/referrals/referral-worthy-platform-features-report.md

as the source proposal. The document identifies high-impact features intended to make Stoquify a differentiated, sticky, referral-worthy SMB operations platform across accounting, inventory, POS, payroll, compliance, reporting, business control, cash leakage prevention, accountant collaboration, and financing readiness.

Your task is to architect and design a complete execution roadmap that turns those proposals into a practical, phased product strategy for Stoquify.

Use all relevant specialized agents, skills, and expert perspectives available, including product strategy, SaaS architecture, UX design, accounting/OHADA compliance, inventory control, POS operations, payroll, business intelligence, fraud prevention, AI copilot design, customer success, go-to-market, pricing, and engineering execution planning.

Do not simply list features. Build a serious execution blueprint that can guide implementation.

Structure the roadmap as follows:

1. Strategic North Star
   - Define what Stoquify should become.
   - Explain the product positioning that will make it stand out from ordinary accounting, POS, ERP, and inventory tools.
   - Translate the report's ideas into a clear market thesis.

2. Feature Pillars
   - Organize the proposed features into major product pillars.
   - For each pillar, explain the user pain, target users, business value, technical requirements, and expected retention/referral effect.

3. Execution Phases
   - Design a phased roadmap: immediate, short-term, medium-term, and long-term.
   - Each phase should include features, dependencies, success criteria, risks, and required data models/workflows.
   - Prioritize based on impact, implementation complexity, revenue potential, and referral potential.

4. Product Architecture
   - Describe the platform capabilities required to support the roadmap.
   - Include modules, data flows, integrations, permissions, audit trails, reporting layers, notifications, automation, AI copilot touchpoints, and mobile/WhatsApp extensions.
   - Highlight where service-owned truth, evidence, redaction, RBAC, and compliance controls are required.

5. User Experience Design
   - Define the core daily/weekly/monthly user journeys.
   - Design habit loops for owners, managers, cashiers, accountants, payroll operators, and suppliers/customers.
   - Explain what the dashboard, action center, close pack, fraud radar, financing passport, and collaboration surfaces should feel like.

6. Differentiation And Moat
   - Explain how Stoquify can become hard to replace.
   - Identify data moats, workflow moats, trust moats, compliance moats, collaboration moats, and network/referral loops.
   - Show how the product becomes more valuable as more users, accountants, suppliers, employees, and customers interact with it.

7. Monetization And Packaging
   - Recommend feature packaging across free, starter, professional, premium, accountant-firm, and enterprise tiers.
   - Explain which features should drive upgrades.
   - Suggest pricing logic, add-ons, and partner opportunities.

8. Go-To-Market And Referral Strategy
   - Define how each feature can create natural referrals.
   - Include accountant-led distribution, supplier/customer sharing, financing partnerships, digital receipt visibility, WhatsApp workflows, and business proof packs.
   - Recommend launch narratives and sales angles.

9. Implementation Backlog
   - Convert the roadmap into an actionable backlog.
   - Include epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, and suggested sequencing.
   - Identify the top 10 implementation tasks to start with.

10. Success Metrics
   - Define product, retention, revenue, referral, operational trust, and compliance metrics.
   - Include daily active usage, close completion, reconciliation rate, leakage detected, accountant invites, referral conversion, paid upgrades, and financing-readiness adoption.

11. Risks And Guardrails
   - Identify execution risks, compliance risks, data-quality risks, AI/copilot risks, operational risks, and adoption risks.
   - Recommend safeguards and validation gates.

Be practical, specific, and implementation-oriented. Think deeply about African/OHADA SMB realities: cash-heavy operations, mobile money, weak records, inventory leakage, payroll trust, tax anxiety, accountant dependency, financing difficulty, low tolerance for complexity, and the need for visible business professionalism.

The final output should read like a serious product execution roadmap that Stoquify's founder, engineers, designers, and go-to-market team can use to build a platform that becomes indispensable and meaningfully differentiated.
```
