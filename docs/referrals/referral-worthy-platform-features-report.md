# Referral-Worthy Platform Features Report

The strongest path is not to make Stoquify "accounting software with AI." Make it the daily operating truth system for SMBs. The copilot and WhatsApp are channels; the real stickiness comes from proof, control, cash confidence, and collaboration.

## 1. High-Impact Feature Ideas

1. **Daily Business Truth Dashboard**
   A morning command center showing yesterday's sales, cash collected, stock risks, unpaid invoices, expenses, payroll alerts, compliance deadlines, and what needs action today.

2. **Cash Leakage & Fraud Radar**
   Automatically compare POS sales, mobile money/bank payments, cash drawer, stock movement, refunds, discounts, voids, and staff activity. Flag suspicious gaps, such as: "Sales say 250,000 XAF, payments received 213,000 XAF."

3. **Accountant Collaboration Portal**
   Give accountants a clean monthly close workspace: missing receipts, unreconciled payments, tax-ready summaries, OHADA/SYSCOHADA reports, evidence packs, and client review notes.

4. **Customer & Supplier Statement Hub**
   Let businesses send professional statements, invoices, debt reminders, delivery confirmations, purchase orders, and supplier reconciliation links.

5. **Business Health Score & Financing Passport**
   Turn clean operating data into a lender-ready trust pack: revenue trend, stock value, payment discipline, expense ratio, tax compliance, payroll consistency, and fraud controls.

6. **Inventory Loss & Anti-Theft Controls**
   Cycle counts, stock variance reports, transfer approvals, damaged/expired stock tracking, staff accountability, and loss by product/location/person views.

7. **Compliance Calendar & Filing Readiness**
   Country-specific reminders for tax, payroll, social contributions, OHADA accounting close, business licenses, declarations, and penalties avoided.

8. **Approval Workflows**
   Owner approval for discounts, refunds, cash withdrawals, supplier payments, stock write-offs, payroll changes, and high-value purchases.

9. **Customer Loyalty & Digital Receipt Engine**
   Digital receipts, loyalty points, customer purchase history, birthday offers, warranty proof, after-sale messages, and simple campaigns.

10. **Peer Benchmarking**
    Anonymous comparisons such as: "Your stock turnover is slower than similar shops," "Your payroll cost is high for your revenue band," or "Your cash leakage risk is low."

## 2. Stickiness And Habit Loops

The daily habit should be: the owner opens Stoquify every morning to answer, "Am I safe today?"

The strongest loops:

- **Morning truth loop:** sales, cash, stock, alerts, tasks.
- **End-of-day close loop:** cashier reconciliation, cash count, mobile money match, manager sign-off.
- **Weekly stock discipline loop:** top losses, low-stock items, dead stock, reorder suggestions.
- **Monthly accountant loop:** close checklist, evidence pack, tax-ready reports.
- **Payroll loop:** attendance, advances, deductions, approvals, payslip release.
- **Collections loop:** unpaid customers, reminder schedule, promise-to-pay tracking.

## 3. Viral / Referral Triggers

The features that naturally spread the product:

- **Accountant portal:** accountants invite all their SMB clients.
- **Digital receipts:** customers see the business as more professional.
- **Supplier statements:** suppliers experience faster reconciliation and ask who made it.
- **Financing passport:** lenders, MFIs, and banks become distribution partners.
- **Employee self-service:** staff use payslips, schedules, advances, and attendance proof.
- **Customer loyalty:** buyers get receipts, points, reminders, and offers.
- **Close pack export:** owners proudly share bank-ready or tax-ready business proof.

The emotional trigger is not "nice software." It is: "This system made my business look serious."

## 4. Differentiation

Most SMB tools record transactions. Stoquify should prove business truth.

Position it as:

- **The anti-leakage operating system** for cash-heavy SMBs.
- **The accountant-ready business brain** for OHADA markets.
- **The owner's daily control room**, not just back-office software.
- **A trust passport** for financing, suppliers, investors, and tax readiness.
- **Offline/mobile-money aware**, built for real African operating conditions.

This matters because World Bank Global Findex 2025 highlights mobile access and digital finance as central infrastructure in developing economies, while OHADA's framework makes accounting/compliance localization a real moat.

## 6. Product Packaging

- **Free / Starter:** digital receipts, basic sales dashboard, simple inventory, limited customer statements.
- **Core Paid:** daily truth dashboard, POS, inventory, basic accounting, customer/supplier balances.
- **Professional:** leakage radar, approvals, accountant portal, monthly close pack, payroll controls.
- **Premium:** financing passport, advanced analytics, multi-location controls, automated reconciliation.
- **Enterprise / Accountant Firm:** multi-client dashboard, staff roles, audit logs, compliance packs, API/export integrations.

The biggest pricing power sits in features that protect money: leakage detection, reconciliation, compliance readiness, accountant collaboration, and financing proof. People pay more when the product helps them recover cash, avoid penalties, access credit, or control staff risk.
