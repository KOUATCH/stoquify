## Harmonization decisions

- October equipment information governs the current operating layout; conflicting mixer ratings and missing model plates remain unresolved.
- H01–H05 centralize handling: cold storage target 4°C or below, frozen stock −18°C or colder, hot holding at least 63°C, meat/fish/poultry/cooked-egg cooking and reheating 75°C for 30 seconds. Product-specific milk treatment and yogurt incubation remain separate. External guidance is not represented as Cameroonian law.
- Continuous dairy treatment seconds were not transferred to an ordinary pot. The batch reference remains 69°C/30 minutes in a qualified system; approved ready mix is the fallback.
- A seven-day frozen pilot suggestion was removed as an automatic sale period. No generic commercial juice or yogurt shelf life was invented.
- Current Yogourmet guidance specifies one sachet per litre; the retained 6 g source entry is conditional on verifying the local sachet mass and identity.
- O and S prefixes prevent conflicting B01/P01/F01 identifiers from being mistaken for the same formula.
- O-B32 is identified as a batter-to-baked-sponge component; assembled cake uses baked sponge output.
- O-F07 conservative 12-bar costing is distinguished from the arithmetic maximum of 13 × 70 g from 970 g usable mix.
- Agege improver dose, Shokupan liquid allocation, levain hydration, unidentified stabilizers, unnamed EDC and missing pastry/filling quantities remain visible gaps.
- Koki corn and makabo are not treated as interchangeable raw materials. Avocado antibrowning, garlic/oil sauce, caramel and hot-filled condiments are not described as shelf-stable.
- Solar electricity and residual oven heat are not free operating costs. Supplier and market claims remain hypotheses.
- Generic repeated controls, duplicated publications, historic build logs, unsupported promotional language and superseded prose were consolidated; no source files were edited.
