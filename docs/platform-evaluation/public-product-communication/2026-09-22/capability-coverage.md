# Public product communication: capability and claim evidence

Assessment: 22 September 2026. Scope: the current shared working tree and the English/French landing, workflows, login and registration experiences. This is a source review, not a production availability, statutory, security or release certification. No business transaction or external message was created for this review. Existing unrelated working-tree changes were preserved.

## Evidence and status rules

- **Implemented** means the behavior is present in current source. It does not establish that every deployed tenant has the feature enabled.
- **Conditional** means implemented behavior requires module access, permissions, setup, current evidence or a supported external source.
- **Partial** means a narrower implemented workflow exists while an important end-to-end or country/provider capability remains unavailable, gated or unverified.
- **Proposed** means a roadmap or conceptual journey without sufficient implementation evidence; do not present it as available.
- Test files cited below are existing verification targets, not tests rerun by this source review. Current execution and browser evidence belongs in the implementation verification report.

All public outcomes should be qualitative: help the team follow records, review differences, prepare work and understand responsibilities. Do not claim measured savings, universal accuracy, universal provider connectivity, automatic statutory acceptance or certified compliance.

## Audience and page purpose

The primary audience is owners and managers of retail and distribution businesses in OHADA markets. That describes the target market, not a promise of statutory coverage across all OHADA countries. Cashiers, stock and purchasing teams, finance/accounting teams, HR/payroll teams and administrators need distinct entry points into the same connected story.

| Page | Educational purpose | Appropriate depth |

## Recommended connected explanation

Use these as public explanations with the prerequisites above, not as a claim that every action is automatic:

1. **Sell and collect:** cashier records sale → the system links tracked stock and recorded payment/accounting entries → cashier reviews drawer → finance compares available payment evidence and reviews differences.
2. **Purchase and receive:** purchasing prepares order → authorized reviewer approves → receiving records accepted goods → stock updates from accepted receipt → finance checks supplier invoice and payment readiness.
3. **Move and review stock:** stock team requests/records movement → required approver reviews → source/destination movements retain their references → manager reviews quantity/value differences.
4. **Follow customer collections:** on-account sale creates a customer obligation → finance records collection and settlement evidence → outstanding position and differences remain reviewable.
5. **Prepare people and payroll:** HR reviews identity, contract, compensation and time inputs → payroll readiness checks expose missing information → authorized payroll review controls run/payslip/accounting stages within supported country scope.
6. **Review and close:** operating records support journals → accountant reviews reconciliations and evidence gaps → responsible teams resolve or respond → the reviewer assembles the internal close evidence pack.
7. **Run the business each day:** managers review current scoped signals → open the original record → assign work or review a decision → return to the operational workflow.

For every visual connection, label what passes: sale reference, stock movement, accepted receipt, invoice, customer obligation, payment proof, reviewed employee inputs or journal reference. Mark meaningful actor changes and human approval steps in plain language. “Recorded”, “reviewed”, “released” and “settled” must not be used interchangeably.

## Source-verifiable limitations and exclusions

1. **Approved policy destinations are missing in the current source tree.** `components/auth/BeautifulRegisterForm.tsx:533,537` links localized `/terms` and `/privacy`. No corresponding route files were found under `app`, and no terms/privacy rewrite was found in `middleware.ts` or `next.config.mjs`. This is source evidence of a pre-existing destination gap; live route status needs browser verification. Preserve required acceptance and request approved policy content through a separate follow-up rather than inventing legal text.
2. **Real email delivery was not exercised.** Registration calls `sendVerificationEmail` and returns `verificationRequired: true`; this establishes intended sequence only. Mailbox delivery, real new-account onboarding and live login success need environment-specific evidence.
3. **Country and payroll breadth is limited.** The static country registry lists Cameroon; fiscal adapters are sandbox only, and an IRPP full-production blocker remains explicit. OHADA audience positioning must not become a promise of every member country's payroll/tax support.
4. **External settlement and authority acceptance are unproven here.** Public explanation can describe records and evidence review. It cannot claim live universal connectors, payment execution or acceptance by authorities.
5. **Offline and assistance are conditional extensions.** Their supported states belong in deeper explanations, with local qualifications. Do not headline “always offline” or “autonomous finance.”
6. **Roadmap-only ideas are excluded from available-feature claims.** No proposed module was promoted based only on historical reports or graph nodes. If retained for future design, label it proposed in internal artifacts and keep it out of present-tense public capability claims.
7. **The embedded July 2026 product image remains historical evidence.** The earlier claims assessment records the historical screenshot date as 18 July 2026. Do not call it live data or current customer results; preserve visible bilingual illustration/history labeling if reused.
