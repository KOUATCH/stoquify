# X1 approval-system record ID and contract artifact URI guide

Date: 2026-08-31

## Plain-English definitions

**Approval-system record ID** is the immutable identifier created by the system where an authorized human actually makes and signs the decision. It proves who decided, under what authority, what was approved, when it applies, when it expires, and which exact document and contract hashes were reviewed. It cannot be obtained from the Stoquify application database and must not be invented.

**X1 contract artifact URI** is the stable location of the exact technical contract package reviewed by the approver. For these policies the repository-derived URI is:

`repository://stoquify/docs/blockers-docs/X1_CONTRACT_ARTIFACT_MANIFEST_2026-08-31.json`

Its SHA-256 is `cdb16e90a74ea942e204bc8191445041a98102850619363096786768e777c9e4`.

## Where to obtain the approval-system record ID

1. Use your organization's authoritative approval platform: GRC/control system, change-management system, governed ticket system, or signed document-workflow system.
2. Create a separate controlled decision for each policy unless your governance system explicitly supports one parent approval with three independently signed child scopes.
3. Record the policy ID, tenant `rds_org_001`, non-production scope, authority appointment reference, explicit decision, effective/expiry dates, contract URI/hash, final DOCX hash, and signature reference.
4. Have the formally appointed authority authenticate and submit the decision.
5. Copy the immutable record ID generated by that platform into the matching DOCX completion table.

## Important boundary

The hashes prove file integrity only. They do not prove approval, authority, signature validity, credential authorization, tenant authorization, release authorization, or production readiness.
