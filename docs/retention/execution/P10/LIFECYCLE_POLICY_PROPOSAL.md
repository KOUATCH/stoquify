## Proposed operating model

Keep three distinct records: **commercial agreement/state**, **technical admission/continuation status**, and **archive/deletion case**. Security identity/consent remains an independent gate. Until a production lifecycle owner exists, propose a controlled case register with organization ID, authenticated requester, agreement version, request/effective dates, state before/after, decision owner/reviewer, scoped access decision, outstanding jobs/offline inventory, source evidence, communications receipt, next review and recovery action. No passwords, payment credentials or raw customer payloads in that register. The system of record and assigned operator are decisions D1/D7, not implemented by this draft.

Cancellation is an instruction, not a retention interview. Feedback, reason selection, a sales call, an invoice dispute, an export download, or clearing operational obligations must **not** be prerequisites for accepting a valid cancellation. Record its agreed effective billing date separately from technical cleanup. Do not continue charging because the platform cannot stop a module or generate an archive. Any final invoice/refund follows explicitly accepted terms and reviewed facts, not this document or a support agent's guess.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| T5 past_due → active | Commercial finance verifies settlement/correction and owner confirms service continuation | Clear arrears/dispute as evidenced; preserve history | Resume only entitled scope after fresh readiness/access review | Duplicate/late signals do not re-bill or undo accepted cancellation; mismatch remains investigated. |

The transition table is complete as a proposed specification, not authorization to execute any transition now.

## Candidate timing choices for review

Concrete **unapproved defaults**, not a contract or legal minimum: a 14-calendar-day evaluation starting on a mutually agreed data-ready date; a 7-calendar-day payment grace after a verified notice; a 90-calendar-day authenticated archive access window after service ends. Choose actual dates/timezone in the signed scope. Cancellation receipt should be acknowledged within one staffed business day; action timestamp must preserve when the valid request arrived. Customers may request an earlier/later effective date subject to reviewed accepted terms, without a mandatory reason.

Archive access expiry must not mean source deletion. If Stoquify prevents an authorized retrieval, propose extending the access window through resolution and a reasonable retrieval opportunity at no renewal requirement; actual extension terms need approval. Artifact TTL may remain short (current inventory default 24 hours/max 7 days), with reissue under fresh authority. The 90-day proposal is not a statutory retention period. Qualified review must determine applicable source retention, legal holds, backup treatment and erasure scope by data class and jurisdiction. No legal/tax rule is asserted in P10.
