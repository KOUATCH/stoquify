## Current hard constraints are gaps, not retention benefits

- Customer rows and customer-order exports cap at **5,000**, fail above cap (`customer.service.ts:126,612,675`). Customer export redacts email, phone, address, tax ID, credit limit, current balance and notes (`customer-export.service.ts:68`). It also selects active/nondeleted scope; archive retention must not silently inherit a report's omissions.
- AP history calls `readAPHistory(... exportRowLimit: 5000)`, rejects pagination/count disagreement, rejects content over **8 MiB**, runs a **20-second RepeatableRead transaction**, and labels the financial basis current operational filtered invoices (`ap-history-export.service.ts:71–88`). It is not a complete supplier balance or certified historical trial balance. Row/byte/time caps require a separately verified archive extraction strategy, not instructions that customers endlessly split reports.
- Inventory background export permits at most **250,000 rows**, encrypted/chunked artifacts and explicit retention/expiry (`inventory-history-background-export.service.ts:50–84,164`). Current source search finds the streaming helper used by tests, not wired into an app/action download endpoint. Artifact infrastructure alone proves no downloaded archive.
- Close pack source redacts sensitive metadata and person-level payroll, emits evidence/source references, current-proof state, reviews/comments and summaries. A usable pack does not prove that source attachments and all underlying records are delivered.
- Read-only entitlement restrictions and inconsistent export admission are policy/implementation constraints to resolve fairly. None is a defensible customer-value moat.
