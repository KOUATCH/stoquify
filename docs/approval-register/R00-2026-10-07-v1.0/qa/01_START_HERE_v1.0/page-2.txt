How to validate and return the pack
1 Read the brief
Open document 02. Read the confirmed facts, proposal, alternatives and due gate for each decision. A proposed
recommendation is not your selected decision.
2 Complete the response
Answer every numbered question. Enter the exact selected option and rationale. Use “not yet available” for
missing facts; do not invent thresholds, legal periods or reviewer names.
3 Check supporting evidence
Open each cited item. Check scope, date, version and whether it actually supports the choice. Ask for
demonstrations or worked examples where a policy affects calculations or workflow.
4 Appoint reviewers
Use document 03. Establish names, relevant qualifications, authority, scope, validity and conflicts. Obtain
acceptance. If a role is missing, assign someone to obtain it and record the capability restriction.
5 Obtain scoped conclusions
Give the relevant specialist the sheet and evidence. Ask what is correct, uncertain and missing, what cases were
checked and what would invalidate their conclusion. Do not turn a technical review into legal certification.
6 Record the outcome
Use document 04: approve, reject or request revision. Conditional approval requires named conditions, owners
and due gates and is not final acceptance. A deferral/not-applicable decision needs a reason and capability
restriction.
7 Freeze before signing
Resolve edits, identify final revision, filenames and pages, and freeze the evidence bundle. A custodian can
calculate a SHA-256 file digest to detect later changes. A digest does not prove identity or approval.
8 Sign and verify
Use the adopted internal method. Paper: sign the exact final pages, retain the original, scan and verify
identity/authority through an established channel. Native confirmation may be used only if the deployed register
and verification process are available and qualified; its availability is not established here.
9 Return privately
Place completed documents and authority evidence in an access-controlled approved repository. Send only a
secure reference to the custodian. Do not commit identity scans, signatures, private employee data or credentials
to the public code repository.
10 Reconcile and maintain
Custodian checks exact version, scope, signer authority and verification, then records the decision. Keep signing,
receipt and verification dates separate. Changes or revoked authority require a new review; retain
superseded/revoked history.
