Start with the decision workbook. Confirm what the first supported product will include, appoint real reviewers,
and record each decision against an exact version. These are internal governance templates, not statutory forms.
No signature or approval has been supplied.
Document Use and who completes it
02 Decision workbook Owner drafts answers to D01–D08; each relevant specialist reviews their scope.
One brief and one response page per decision.
03 Reviewer appointments Owner/delegator proposes named appointments; appointees accept and a
verifier checks authority and independence.
04 Approval record Duplicate for each decision and signing authority. Reviewers record conclusions;
authorized decision maker records the outcome; verification follows.
05 Evidence and open items Delivery lead tracks evidence, missing people/answers, restrictions and R00
acceptance. This document itself needs no signature.
What you can decide yourself
If you hold the relevant project/business authority, you can choose the target customer and product scope (D01),
optional priorities (D06), propose appointments and accept commercial constraints. Final D01/D06 decisions still
require joint concurrence from the roles in document 03. Your authority is not established by this pack; complete
the authority fields. D03 and D08 require operational/security or release input. D07 requires SRE and finance
input.
What needs another reviewer
D02 needs qualified accounting review. D04 needs privacy/records, HR and security review. D05 needs qualified
Cameroon expertise and provider/submission authority for each capability. Independent technical review cannot
be supplied by a second account for the implementer. Compatible roles may be combined only where the
adopted policy and stricter domain controls permit it. [S5, S6]
What you sign
First confirm appointments in document 03 after authority checks; each appointee accepts. Then use document
04 for the exact completed decision sheet and its evidence. Do not sign a blank proposal or approve all eight
decisions through an unexplained blanket signature. The guide and checklist are working aids.
