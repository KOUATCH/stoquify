D01 Product scope and first customers
Canonical definition: Initial segment, locations, currencies, enabled modules and capability promises. [S1]
Choose who the first supported product is for and what you will promise them. A country choice alone does not
select the customer type or authorize a live launch.
Already confirmed
Cameroon first, later CEMAC expansion, pre-production/demo data and phased implementation are confirmed.
Customer sector, entity type, site count, currencies and first-release modules are not yet selected. [S3]
Proposal for your review
Proposed: select one defined Cameroon customer cohort and qualify online workflows first. Treat accounting and
HR/payroll as separately gated capabilities. Use synthetic demonstrations until the relevant acceptance evidence
is available.
Alternatives and consequences
A narrow cohort limits test combinations and support cost. Multiple sectors/sites at launch increase configuration,
training and regression coverage. Do not assume every implemented module is promised.
Evidence to inspect
Customer needs or pilot brief; entity and location inventory; capability matrix; current package acceptance and
exclusions. [S1, S2, S8]
Decision timing and deferral
Individual capabilities may be excluded with a reason and owner. The target cohort and explicit release boundary
cannot remain ambiguous when R00 is accepted.
Accountable roles: Product owner + delivery lead. Review: QA lead; domain owners for every promised capability.
Due gate: R00 before the release-scope baseline; R33 still controls actual release.
