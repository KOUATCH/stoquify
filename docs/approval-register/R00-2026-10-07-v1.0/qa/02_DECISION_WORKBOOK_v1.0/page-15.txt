D08 Pilot admission and observation
Canonical definition: Pilot cohorts, minimum volume, business-cycle coverage and observation window. [S1]
Define a fair pilot test in advance: participants, duration, business cycles, acceptance evidence and stop
conditions.
Already confirmed
Cameroon is prioritized. No named pilot customer, transaction-volume threshold, observation window or pilot
authorization has been confirmed. [S3]
Proposal for your review
Proposed: admit a bounded cohort only after its applicable security, data, country and provider gates pass. Agree
the pilot criteria before seeing the results; keep online and offline admission distinct.
Alternatives and consequences
A shorter/narrower pilot costs less but cannot demonstrate unobserved business cycles. Broader admission needs
more support and evidence. A synthetic rehearsal is useful but does not establish real-customer pilot
performance.
Evidence to inspect
Named cohort and permitted capabilities; signed criteria; support/rollback plan; representative business-cycle
evidence; gate matrix and independent review. [S1, S2]
Decision timing and deferral
Pilot may be deferred while engineering continues. No admission or release approval follows from a draft plan or
successful rehearsal.
Accountable roles: Release owner + pilot operations. Review: Product, QA and relevant domain owners;
country/provider reviewers for regulated or external capabilities.
Due gate: Before R32/R33; criteria must not be invented after results.
