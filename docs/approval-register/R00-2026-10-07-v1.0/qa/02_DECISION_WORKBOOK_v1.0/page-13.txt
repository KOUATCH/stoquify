D07 Operational service objectives
Canonical definition: Deployment workload, latency, error, backlog, cost, RPO/RTO and alert objectives. [S1]
Agree the operating conditions and measurable service limits before judging operational readiness. RPO means
acceptable data-loss window; RTO means acceptable recovery time.
Already confirmed
Current evidence does not qualify a fresh integrated build/package, complete browser journeys or production
recovery. No measured production service objectives are approved here. [S2, S3]
Proposal for your review
Proposed: first measure representative workloads in a controlled environment, then agree explicit thresholds and
measurement windows with product, finance and SRE. Do not publish unmeasured service promises.
Alternatives and consequences
Tighter latency/recovery targets generally require more capacity, recovery automation and support expenditure.
A smaller bounded cohort may reduce the initial operating envelope but still needs measured acceptance.
Evidence to inspect
Deployment topology; workload model; reproducible baseline measurements; restore drill; worker/backlog
monitoring; alert delivery and cost model. [S1, S2]
Decision timing and deferral
Leave unknown thresholds blank with a measurement owner/date. That service promise remains unqualified.
Record zero silent event loss, duplicate economic effects and unauthorized access as proposed invariants, not
measured achievements.
Accountable roles: SRE + product + finance. Review: Product owner and finance owner; independent QA/technical
evidence review.
Due gate: R23/R30 before measuring operational acceptance against agreed limits.
