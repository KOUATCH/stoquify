D04 Access privacy and retention
Canonical definition: Role matrix, legal holds, retention and HR/workforce disclosure. [S1]
Specify who can see and change each class of data, what must be retained, and how sensitive workforce
information is protected.
Already confirmed
Current work uses test/demo data. No production retention schedule, complete access matrix or employee
disclosure policy is approved in this pack. [S3]
Proposal for your review
Proposed: use minimum role-based access, explicit employee/manager/accountant scopes and controlled export
permissions. Keep payroll details restricted and define legal-hold precedence before adopting deletion schedules.
Alternatives and consequences
Broad convenience access increases disclosure risk and review burden. More granular access requires role testing
and support procedures. Retention periods must follow entity/data applicability review, not a guessed universal
duration.
Evidence to inspect
Data inventory and classification; role/tenant/action matrix; employee disclosure rules; retention and hold
sources; export and access-revocation tests. [S1, S2]
Decision timing and deferral
Missing policy blocks affected sensitive use or deletion. Record the exact restricted data/actions and reviewer due
date; pre-production status alone does not prove every record is synthetic.
Accountable roles: Privacy/records + HR + AppSec. Review: Application security; qualified legal/privacy advice for
applicable retention and disclosure duties.
Due gate: R08, R10, R16 and R28 before enforcement acceptance and sensitive launch.
