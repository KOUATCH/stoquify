D03 Offline operating limits
Canonical definition: Offline devices, storage/eviction, supported tenders, fiscal limits and recovery. [S1]
Decide whether offline selling is in scope and the exact devices, payments and recovery limits that must be
proven before admitting it.
Already confirmed
The current continuation does not qualify a complete offline pilot. No supported device/tender matrix is approved
here. [S2, S3]
Proposal for your review
Proposed: defer live offline admission until R04/R05 data-conservation evidence and R32 device/pilot criteria pass.
Continue controlled engineering tests with synthetic data.
Alternatives and consequences
Online-only admission has a narrower recovery surface. A controlled offline pilot adds device enrollment, storage
loss, retries, uncertain payments, fiscal and support obligations. Browser storage alone is not a durability
guarantee.
Evidence to inspect
Browser/OS/device matrix; capacity and eviction tests; replay/conservation tests; tender/provider/fiscal review;
operator recovery drill. [S1, S2]
Decision timing and deferral
If offline is excluded, record that decision and prohibit offline capability promises/admission. Reopen D03 before
enabling it; do not mark the capability verified.
Accountable roles: POS/product + security + country owner. Review: Security owner and country owner; QA and
support for device/recovery evidence.
Due gate: R04/R05 design; finalize before R32 offline admission.
