D02 Accounting policy choices
Canonical definition: Functional currency, rate/rounding, partial-year, close and year-end policies. [S1]
Agree the accounting rules against which the implementation and reports will be assessed. A green test is not an
accountant approving the underlying policy.
Already confirmed
Cameroon is the first jurisdiction. No functional-currency policy, rate source, rounding method, fiscal calendar or
close policy has been approved in this pack. [S3, S4]
Proposal for your review
Proposed: have the accounting owner and qualified accountant document the policy for the selected entity and
reporting framework, using explicit examples. Keep unsupported currency/reporting cases unavailable until
mappings and source amounts are verified.
Alternatives and consequences
Single-currency scope reduces conversion cases. Multi-currency scope requires approved rate authority, dates,
missing-rate handling and reproducible conversion/rounding. Close-only locking and professionally qualified close
have different evidence requirements.
Evidence to inspect
Entity/framework applicability; rate-source evidence; calculation examples; fiscal-year setup; journal/report tie-
outs; close, reopening and correction controls. [S1, S2, S4]
Decision timing and deferral
The detailed policy may remain pending during development, with a named reviewer and due gate. Affected
financial outputs cannot be represented as qualified.
Accountable roles: Accounting owner + qualified accountant. Review: Qualified accountant; engineering verifies
the implementation against approved examples.
Due gate: R02, R06, R07 and R20 before financial implementation acceptance.
