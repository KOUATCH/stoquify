Meaning of the approval
The decision approves only the identified scope/policy proposal. It does not assert that the implementation meets
it. It does not authorize production deployment, real payments or statutory filing, and does not certify Cameroon
or OHADA compliance. Expert conclusions remain limited to their explicit reviewed scope.
