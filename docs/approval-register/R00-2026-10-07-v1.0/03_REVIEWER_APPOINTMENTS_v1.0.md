Authority and independence

An owner may also act as product/delivery lead if their authority covers both. Product and security separation is the existing proposed default; independent technical review remains required. Combine other roles only after documenting competence, authority, conflicts and applicable domain restrictions. A creator cannot satisfy an independent review requirement by self-signing under a different title. [S5, S6]

Authority may be supported by ownership records, an executive/board mandate, a contract or a scoped appointment. A personally owned project may need an owner-establishment declaration supported by ownership evidence and independent verification. Qualification and delegated authority are separate checks. Use secure references, not attached private identity documents.

Assess maker/checker, signer/verifier, evidence producer/reviewer and personal/financial conflicts. The subject must not approve their own conflict assessment. Delegated powers cannot exceed the delegator’s scope or validity. [S6, S10]
