1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT
--------------------

Enterprise transformation program: GO_WITH_GATES
Unrestricted enterprise production claim: NO_GO
Statutory or authority-certified claim: NO_GO
Controlled bounded implementation slices: GO_WITH_GATES

Stoquify already contains serious enterprise financial and operational
foundations:

- tenant-scoped domain services;
- RBAC and fresh-auth seams;
- POS and offline replay controls;
- inventory event posting and reconciliation;
- purchasing/AP maker-checker workflows;
- payment reconciliation and suspense;
- journal, period, ledger, and close-assurance models;
- HRIS-to-payroll proof contracts;
- deterministic hashes, business events, outboxes, audit records, and exports;
- workflow-assurance findings and incidents;
- extensive static gates and focused tests.

Current evidence does not prove that the complete platform is
enterprise-production-ready. Main blockers:

1. Extensive uncommitted, overlapping work means several artifacts are not
   bound to a clean authoritative revision.
2. Module entitlements are derived from legacy/requested-module data rather
   than a durable commercial source of truth.
3. Country-pack readiness is blocked by source-artifact hash verification and
   expert approval.
4. Production-secret readiness is conditional.
5. HRIS still uses payroll-named compatibility storage for important facts.
6. Cross-domain close invalidation is not proven complete for every writer.
7. Provider, authority, hardware, backup/restore, deployment, alert, and legal
   evidence remain external.
8. Architecture graphs predate the latest agent-runtime work.

5. CROSS-DOMAIN PROOF CHAINS
-----------------------------

POS -> Inventory -> Payment -> Ledger -> Fiscal -> Close

Strengths:
- tenant scope, transactions, business events, outboxes, hashes, replay checks;
- deterministic inventory issue/return keys;
- offline device, sequence, hash-chain, quarantine, and exact-once controls;
- accounting and close seams.

Unproven:
- real devices and connectivity;
- live card/mobile-money settlement;
- fiscal authority delivery;
- full offline-to-close chaos proof;
- complete close invalidation after every correction.

Purchasing -> Receipt -> Stock -> Invoice -> Match -> AP -> Payment ->
Reconciliation -> Close

Strengths:
- maker-checker;
- atomic receipt/stock posting;
- three-way match;
- bank-destination approval;
- separate requester, approver, and releaser;
- hashes, events, ledger, reconciliation, and close seams.

Unproven:
- supplier document authenticity;
- bank ownership verification;
- live provider execution;
- complete AP mutation-to-close invalidation coverage.

HRIS -> Certified Snapshot -> Payroll -> Payment/Declaration -> Accounting ->
Close

Strengths:
- identity, contract, document, compensation, destination, and attendance proof;
- maker-checker and source hashes;
- fail-closed payroll input;
- payroll immutability;
- hashes, idempotency, events, and outboxes.

Unproven or partial:
- HRIS durable ownership migration;
- refreshed HRIS final readiness;
- country-pack approval;
- live provider/authority integration;
- complete privacy, document-vault, and retention operations.

Certified Evidence Change -> Invalidation -> Blocker -> Correction ->
Refreshed Pack

Strengths:
- multiple domain invalidation services;
- reversals and corrections;
- close source checks.

Gap:
- no current single artifact proves every close-impacting writer is covered.

11. MIGRATION, BACKFILL, AND ROLLBACK
-------------------------------------

Migration:

  metadata, actor, timestamps, and supersession evidence.

Backfill:
1. dry-run legacy/requested state;
2. generate deterministic proposed rows;
3. report unknown slugs and dependency/access changes;
4. require zero unexplained access loss;
5. apply idempotently;
6. reconcile decision equivalence.

Rollback:
- return Analytics pilot to observe mode;
- preserve entitlement and audit rows;
- use forward correction, not destructive rollback.
