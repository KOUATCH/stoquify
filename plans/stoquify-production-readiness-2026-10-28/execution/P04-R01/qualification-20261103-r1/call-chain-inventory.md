## Authority discrepancy

`operating-access-scope-contracts.ts:26-60` recognizes trimmed, lower-case `admin`, `administrator`, and `super_admin`; `resolveOperatingAccessScope` requires `dashboard.read` first, then accepts a recognized code or RBAC wildcard as tenant authority. Otherwise it resolves active same-tenant managed locations. `lib/security/rbac.ts:164-197` reads current tenant roles, expands permissions, and derives wildcard status. `lib/createRoleName.ts:1` lowercases and replaces whitespace with underscores, so the role creation name is security-relevant.

The role, member and invitation mutation guards currently inspect permission lists without the additional authority implied by a role code. An empty reserved role is latent authority: another role can supply `dashboard.read`. The same escalation can occur when a normal role is assigned or edited for a member who already holds an empty reserved role. No production inventory was queried and no conclusion about existing legitimate role ownership is inferred.
